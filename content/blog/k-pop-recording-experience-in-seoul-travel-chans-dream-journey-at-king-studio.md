### The Enduring Legacy of "I Will Go to You Like the First Snow"

Before diving into Chan's remarkable journey, it's essential to understand why this particular song selection has captivated audiences for nearly a decade. Ailee's emotional masterpiece "I Will Go to You Like the First Snow" emerged as part of the Goblin soundtrack in January 2017, quickly becoming one of the most successful K-Drama OSTs in history. The song achieved unprecedented commercial success, topping Korea's Gaon Digital, Download, and Streaming charts throughout 2017, with over 171 million streams that year alone.

What makes this track particularly significant for Taiwan and Hong Kong audiences is its connection to Goblin's phenomenal success across Asia. The drama became the highest-rated K-drama aired in Taiwan, averaging 2.27% viewership with the final episode reaching 5.5%. This remarkable achievement positioned Goblin ahead of legendary dramas like Boys Before Flowers and Coffee Prince, cementing its status as a cultural phenomenon that transcended national boundaries.

The song's enduring popularity stems from its universal themes of eternal love and devotion, enhanced by Ailee's powerful vocal delivery that perfectly captures the drama's emotional depth. Music critics have noted that "I Will Go to You Like the First Snow" functions as the "sadder, winter, immortal cousin" of seasonal hits, with its ability to re-enter music charts every winter season. This longevity explains why the track continues to resonate with international fans, making it an ideal choice for KING STUDIO's professional recording experience.

### Exploring Seoul's Hidden Gems Around Seolleung

After completing her unforgettable KPop experience at KING STUDIO, Chan's Seoul Travel adventure continued with exploration of the vibrant Seolleung area, perfectly positioned for extending her Korea Experience.

**Cultural Heritage at Seonjeongneung Royal Tombs**  
Just minutes from KING STUDIO, the UNESCO World Heritage Site of Seonjeongneung Royal Tombs offers a fascinating contrast to modern K-Pop culture. These meticulously maintained grounds house the final resting places of Joseon Dynasty King Seongjong and King Jungjong, providing Chan and her mother with a serene historical experience amid Seoul's bustling Gangnam district. The beautifully landscaped paths and traditional architecture create perfect photo opportunities while offering insight into Korea's rich royal heritage.

**Culinary Adventures in Gangnam**  
The Seolleung area boasts exceptional dining options that cater to international visitors seeking authentic Korean flavors. For traditional Korean barbecue, Gyeongcheonaein 2237 earned recognition with its 2023 Blue Ribbon award, offering premium Korean beef in an elegant atmosphere. Chan could experience Korea's famous galbi culture while enjoying perfectly marbled meat and traditional banchan side dishes.

For those seeking comfort food, Nongmin Baekam King Sundae provides an authentic taste of Korean street food culture with their renowned sundae-guk (blood sausage soup). This local favorite, popular with office workers, offers generous portions and rich, flavorful broth that represents true Korean home-style cooking.

International visitors can also enjoy fusion dining at establishments like In the Kitcheon, which combines Italian techniques with Korean ingredients, or Five N Two The Burger Journey for American-style gourmet burgers crafted with Korean sensibilities.

**Shopping and Modern Culture**  
The nearby COEX Mall complex provides extensive shopping opportunities, featuring both international brands and unique Korean products perfect for Seoul Travel souvenirs. The massive underground complex includes the famous COEX Aquarium, entertainment facilities, and countless dining options, making it an ideal afternoon destination following a KING STUDIO recording session.

For a more boutique experience, the trendy Garosu-gil area offers tree-lined streets filled with independent fashion stores, art galleries, and Instagram-worthy cafes. This picturesque neighborhood perfectly captures Seoul's modern aesthetic while providing unique shopping opportunities unavailable elsewhere.
