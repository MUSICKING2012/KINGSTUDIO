### Top 6 Seoul Destinations for N.Flying & N.Fia Fans

Planning your Seoul travel itinerary? Here are the must-visit spots that N.Flying fans and N.Fia love most:

**1\. K-Star Road, Gangnam** - Walk among the famous "Gangnamdols" featuring bear-shaped displays of various K-pop groups, perfect for photo opportunities

**2\. COEX K-Pop Square** - Home to the iconic Gangnam Style statue, K-pop idol handprints, and the latest promotional videos

**3\. Hongdae District** - The heart of Seoul's indie music scene where N.Flying's rock sound resonates with local music lovers

**4\. Han River Park** - A favorite filming location for many K-pop music videos and a peaceful retreat for fans

**5\. Myeongdong** - Perfect for K-pop merchandise shopping and experiencing the bustling energy that inspires many songs

**6\. FNC Entertainment Building** - While you can't go inside, N.Fia often visit to show support for N.Flying and other FNC artists
