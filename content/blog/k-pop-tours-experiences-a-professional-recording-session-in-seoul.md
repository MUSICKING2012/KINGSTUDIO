## K-Pop Experiences: Top 5 Attractions Near King Studio in Seolleung for Singaporean Fans

Discover the ultimate “TAEYEON – Blur” recording session at King Studio in Gangnam and explore five must-visit spots around Seolleung to elevate your Seoul itinerary.

**1\. Seonjeongneung Royal Tombs**

Stroll through the serene UNESCO-listed Seonjeongneung complex, home to the Joseon Dynasty’s royal tombs of King Seongjong, Queen Jeonghyeon, and King Jungjong1. Located just 450 m from Seolleung Station Exit 10, it offers a peaceful contrast to the bustling city.

**2\. Starfield Library at COEX Mall**

Experience one of Seoul’s most Instagrammable spaces inside COEX Mall’s Starfield Library, featuring towering bookshelves and cozy reading nooks. It’s a 15-minute walk or a two-stop ride from Seolleung Station, perfect for K-Pop fans seeking a pop-culture oasis.

**3\. Bongeunsa Temple**

Step into Bongeunsa Temple’s tranquil grounds opposite COEX, where century-old pagodas and lotus ponds invite meditation. The temple’s evening lantern-lit ceremonies create a magical ambiance, ideal after your recording session at King Studio (1.5 km from Seolleung Station).

**4\. K-Star Road & GangnamDol Sculptures**

Follow the 1 km K-Star Road from Apgujeong Rodeo Station to Cheongdam, spotting 17 “GangnamDol” bear sculptures adorned with idol imagery, including Girls’ Generation’s Taeyeon. This art walk passes SM’s Global Training Center and JYP Entertainment headquarters, immersing fans in real-life K-Pop landmarks./

**5\. Starfield COEX Aquarium**

Dive into marine wonders at COEX Aquarium, one of Korea’s largest indoor aquariums. Located beneath COEX Mall, it showcases themed zones from Amazon to Deep Blue Sea, offering a family-friendly and colorful detour after your studio adventure (1.3 km from Seolleung Station).
