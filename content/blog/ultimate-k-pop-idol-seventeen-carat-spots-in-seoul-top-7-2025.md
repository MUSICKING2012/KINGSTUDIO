The journey begins not at a grand concert hall, but in a small, cozy cafe, which is a perfect reflection of modern fandom. The pilgrimage has evolved from visiting large-scale, public venues to seeking out small, intimate places that offer a glimpse into an idol's personal life. A visit to L'INCONNU is about experiencing the subtle, everyday joys the members share with us.

This charming, French-style ice cream and dessert shop is a "hidden gem" located in the trendy Dosan Park area. It became an essential stop after Mingyu shared a photo of himself with his beloved dog inside the shop, posing in front of its antique decor. The act of visiting this spot and perhaps ordering the same tropical sundae is a deeply meaningful experience, an act of sharing a moment with your bias, not just visiting a monument to their fame. This illustrates a trend in fandom where the value of a pilgrimage spot is measured less by its grandeur and more by its authenticity and personal connection.

It is a place for CARATs to bond with other fans, celebrating the group's camaraderie and the shared memories that define the fandom.
