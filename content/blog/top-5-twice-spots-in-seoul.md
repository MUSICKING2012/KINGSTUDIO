### Top 5 Chupungryeong Kal Samgyeopsal / 추풍령칼삼겹살

**The 'Behind' Story**

This authentic Korean BBQ spot is legendary among K-Pop circles. TWICE, along with Super Junior, EXO, SHINee, CNBLUE, NCT, ASTRO, and The Boyz, have all visited this Gangnam restaurant. Why? It's located near major entertainment companies, making it a post-practice favorite. The walls are covered with autographed photos from your favorite idols—visual proof that you're eating where K-Pop royalty dines.

**Vibe & Menu**: What to Order: The herb-and-garlic seasoned pork belly (samgyeopsal) is what brings idols back again and again. Unlike typical BBQ joints, Chupungryeong uses special marinade that elevates the experience to another level.

**Atmosphere**: Bustling, authentic, and energetic. This isn't a tourist trap—it's where locals and celebrities genuinely eat. Expect the sizzle of grills, friendly banter, and that unmistakable Korean BBQ aroma.

**Pro Tip:** The restaurant doesn't take reservations, so arrive early (before 18:00) or be prepared to wait on weekends.

Sinsa Garosu-gil represents the sophisticated evolution of TWICE's style journey. This tree-lined European-style boulevard in trendy Apgujeong has been mentioned by members Sana, Mina, and Tzuyu as their favorite shopping and cafe-hopping destination. Fashion-forward members like Tzuyu have been photographed by fans at various boutiques along this street, making it a pilgrimage site for ONCEs interested in TWICE's evolving style.

**Atmosphere**: European elegance meets Seoul sophistication. Tree-lined streets create the perfect backdrop for that Korea experience aesthetic, while boutique shops and independent cafes provide endless exploration opportunities.

### Top 3 Superzip / 슈퍼집

![](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2026/02/-image----8626316.webp)

![](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2026/02/-image----3075013-1.webp)

![](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2026/02/-image----4007395-1.webp)

**The 'Behind' Story**

TWICE's Nayeon personally recommended this spot to fans during fan sign events and V LIVE broadcasts. Her favorite? The triple cheese tteok-bokki and sundae (Korean blood sausage). When an idol recommends a place directly to ONCEs, you know it's special. Superzip isn't just famous for Nayeon—it's become a pilgrimage site for TWICE fans worldwide.

**Vibe & Menu  
What to Order**: Triple Cheese Tteok-bokki (Nayeon's choice) features three types of cheese—cheddar, mozzarella, and sliced cheese—plus parmesan powder on top. It's rich, creamy, and less spicy than traditional versions, making it perfect for international palates. **Alternative Pick**: If you're not a cheese fan, try their garlic tteok-bokki, another local favorite.

**Atmosphere:** rendy with sensational lighting and concrete walls—casual yet modern. They also serve drinks until late, making it perfect for a Seoul Experience night out.

📍Address (Google) : [14 Eonju-ro 130-gil, Gangnam-gu, Seoul](https://maps.app.goo.gl/2wvCecptruoDhWqK7)  
📍Address (Naver) : [서울특별시 강남구 언주로130길 14](https://map.naver.com/p/directions/-/14141824.7185358,4512673.6813709,%EC%B6%94%ED%92%8D%EB%A0%B9%EC%B9%BC%EC%82%BC%EA%B2%B9%EC%82%B4%20%EC%95%95%EA%B5%AC%EC%A0%95%EC%A0%90,12021444,PLACE_POI/-/transit?c=14.00,0,0,0,dh)

### Top1 Seogangsoe Tteokbokki / 서강쇠 떡볶이

![](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2026/02/-image----4493087.webp)

![](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2026/02/-image----1724611.webp)

![](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2026/02/-image----6357095.webp)

**The 'Behind' Story**

Taking our Top 1 spot is another Nayeon favorite—and when TWICE's lead vocalist recommends a tteok-bokki spot twice, you know it's legendary. Seogangsoe represents the quintessential Korean street food experience elevated to perfection. Nayeon has mentioned this restaurant multiple times across different platforms, cementing its status as TWICE favorite places Seoul royalty.

What makes it special? The owner cuts the long rice cakes fresh in front of you, and they're made with 100% rice (not the flour-rice mix) for that impossibly chewy, authentic texture ONCEs (and Nayeon) can't resist.

**Vibe & Menu  
What to Order**: The signature tteok-bokki—watch as they slice the long, perfectly white rice cakes into bite-sized pieces right before cooking them in rich, savory-spicy sauce. The 100% rice composition creates a texture that's softer and chewier than anywhere else.

**Atmosphere**: Authentic, no-frills Korean street food energy. This is real Seoul—not sanitized for tourists. You'll hear locals chatting, smell the irresistible aroma of gochujang sauce, and feel like you've discovered a true hidden gem.

📍Address (Google) : [11, Gangnam-daero 66-gil, Gangnam-gu, Seoul](https://maps.app.goo.gl/JqLFZztmPkKT1kXx8)  
📍Address (Naver) : [서울특별시 강남구 강남대로66길 11](https://map.naver.com/p/directions/-/14140095.1810032,4511955.3794619,Apple%20%EA%B0%80%EB%A1%9C%EC%88%98%EA%B8%B8,1587700406,PLACE_POI/-/transit?c=14.00,0,0,0,dh)

**Why #1**? This spot appears most frequently in TWICE's recommendations, it's authentically Korean, affordable, and gives you genuine Seoul street food culture—not a tourist version. When Nayeon says it's her favorite, she means it.
