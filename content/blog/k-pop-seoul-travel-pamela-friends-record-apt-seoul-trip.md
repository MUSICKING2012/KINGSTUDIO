### Complete Your Seoul Travel: Top 3 European Traveler Favorites

After your incredible recording session, continue your Seoul Travel adventure with these top recommendations from European visitors. These locations consistently rank as favorites among travelers from Italy, Portugal, and across Europe.

**Top 3 Gwangjang Market: Authentic Korean Culinary Adventure**

![](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2026/03/-image----4604622-1.webp)

![](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2026/03/-image----9408376.webp)

![](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2026/03/-image----3315416.webp)

This historic market offers the most authentic Korean food experience in Seoul. Unlike tourist-focused restaurants, Gwangjang Market serves locals and visitors alike with traditional dishes perfected over generations.

Must-try experiences: Bindaetteok (mung bean pancakes), mayak gimbap (addictive mini rice rolls), and freshly made tteokbokki. The market's bustling atmosphere captures Seoul's vibrant food culture perfectly.

European traveler insight: Come hungry and adventurous. Many vendors now have picture menus, making ordering accessible even with limited Korean language skills. Evening visits offer the most magical atmosphere with warm, inviting lighting.

**Top 2 Bukchon Hanok Village: Where Traditional Korea Meets Modern Seoul**

![](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2026/03/-image----6868969.webp)

![](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2026/03/-image----5250350.webp)

Walking through Bukchon Hanok Village feels like time travel. Traditional Korean houses (hanoks) line narrow alleys, creating endless photo opportunities. The contrast between historic structures and the modern Seoul skyline perfectly represents Korea's balance of tradition and innovation.

Experience highlights: Many hanoks function as cultural centers, tea houses, or small museums. You can participate in traditional craft workshops, try on hanbok (Korean traditional clothing), or simply absorb the peaceful atmosphere.

Photography tip: Visit during golden hour for the most stunning shots. The warm light on traditional architecture creates professional-quality images with minimal effort.
