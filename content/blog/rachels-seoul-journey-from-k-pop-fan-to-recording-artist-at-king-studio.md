Based on Rachel's demo, the vocal director analyzed her voice and recommended three carefully selected songs. And here's where the magic happened: one of the recommendations was **"I Want That Kind of Love (난 그런 사랑을 원해)"** by Kim Soo-yeon from the iconic Korean musical "Hymn to Death (사의 찬미)."

**About "I Want That Kind of Love":**  
This powerful musical number is sung by the character Yun Sim-deok, Korea's first soprano, in the acclaimed Korean musical "Hymn to Death." The song is a passionate declaration of living life fearlessly, embracing love without restraint. With lyrics like "밤에도 낮에도 나 사랑을 속삭일래 (Day and night, I want to whisper love)," it's a bold anthem about seizing the moment—perfect for discovering vocal power and emotional expression.
