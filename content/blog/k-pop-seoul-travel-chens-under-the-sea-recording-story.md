### Complete Your Seoul Travel: Top 3 Taiwan Traveler Favorites

Your K-Pop experience at KING STUDIO deserves to be part of a perfect Korea trip. Based on recommendations from our Taiwan visitors, here are three Seoul destinations receiving consistent rave reviews:

**Top3 Myeongdong Shopping District—K-Beauty Paradise & Street Food Heaven 🛍️**

Myeongdong represents Seoul travel at its most vibrant and accessible. This bustling district combines international brands with uniquely Korean stores, creating retail experiences satisfying every shopping style.

![](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2026/03/456704702_3769337343322633_776282057572416231_n.webp)

*   **Why Taiwan Travelers Love It**: The concentration of cosmetics stores (Innisfree, Etude House, The Face Shop) offers K-beauty products at better prices than international markets. Tax-free shopping adds additional savings, making Myeongdong practical during your Korea trip.
*   **Street Food Excellence**: Between shopping, sample legendary street food—hotteok (sweet pancakes), tornado potatoes, and cheese-covered everything represent Korean street cuisine at its finest.
*   **Evening Magic**: Visit during evening hours when neon signs illuminate and street performers entertain crowds, creating energy that mirrors Taipei's night markets while maintaining distinctly Korean character.

**Top1 Gwangjang Market—The Ultimate Korean Food Adventure 🥢**

Topping Taiwan travelers' recommendations, Gwangjang Market represents the pinnacle of authentic Seoul travel culinary experiences—where locals eat, creating authenticity expensive restaurants cannot replicate.

![](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2026/03/-image----4753463-1.webp)

![](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2026/03/-image----4046271-1.webp)

Why It's #1: Taiwan visitors consistently rate Gwangjang Market as their most memorable Korea trip food experience. The overwhelming variety, affordable prices, and genuine local atmosphere create adventures that define authentic Korea experiences.

**Must-Try Specialties:**

*   Bindaetteok (Mung Bean Pancakes): Crispy, savory pancakes served fresh off the griddle
*   Mayak Gimbap: Addictive mini seaweed rice rolls perfect for market exploration
*   Yukhoe: Fresh beef tartare topped with egg yolk for adventurous eaters
*   Korean Sundae: Blood sausage offering surprisingly mild, savory flavors

Cultural Authenticity: Beyond food, Gwangjang Market offers traditional fabric vendors, vintage clothing sections, and glimpses into daily Korean life rarely seen in tourist districts.
