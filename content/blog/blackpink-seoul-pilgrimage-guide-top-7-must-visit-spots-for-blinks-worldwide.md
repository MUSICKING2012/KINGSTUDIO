### TOP 7: YG Entertainment New Building (YG엔터테인먼트 신사옥)

![](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2025/09/seoul_travel_guide_blackpink_blink-2.webp)

![](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2025/09/seoul_travel_guide_akmu-2.webp)

![](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2025/09/seoul_travel_blackpink_blink-3.webp)

*   **Why it's a Blink Must-Visit:** This is where the magic happens! The heart of YG Entertainment, where **BLACKPINK** trains, records, and creates the hits we all love. While you can't go inside, seeing the iconic building where our idols hone their craft is an essential part of any **K-Pop Trip**. You might even catch a glimpse of them heading to or from work! It's a symbolic starting point for your **BLACKPINK** journey.
*   **Address:**
    *   English: [6-3, Huijeong-ro 1-gil, Mapo-gu, Seoul, Republic of Korea](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&origin=Seoul+Gangnam+Samsung-ro+75-gil+52+B1&destination=YG+Entertainment+New+Building,)
    *   Korean: [서울 마포구 희정로1길 6-3](https://map.naver.com/v5/directions/Seoul%20Gangnam%20Samsung-ro%2075-gil%2052/B1/YG%20엔터테인먼트%20신사옥)
*   **Operating Hours & Holidays:** Exterior viewing is 24/7. Interior access is restricted.
*   **How to Get There from** KING STUDIO**:**
    *   **Public Transportation:** From Samseong Jungang Station (Line 9), transfer to Line 2 at Dangsan Station, and alight at Hapjeong Station. Approximately 1 hour.

### TOP 4: Daelim Changgo Gallery CO:LUMN (대림창고 갤러리 컬럼)

![](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2025/09/seoul_travel_cafe_food.webp)

![](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2025/09/seoul_travel_guide_cafe.webp)

![](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2025/09/seoul_travel_guide.webp)

*   **Why it's a Blink Must-Visit:** Rumored to be a favorite haunt of Jennie (our "Jendeukie"), this unique gallery café in Seongsu-dong is a must-see! With its expansive industrial-chic space and artistic vibe, it’s a place where you can enjoy coffee and brunch while feeling connected to the artistic sensibilities of our **BLACKPINK** members. Seongsu-dong itself is a trendy neighborhood, perfect for exploring more of Seoul's creative side.
*   **Address:**
    *   English: [78, Seongsui-ro, Seongdong-gu, Seoul, Republic of Korea](https://www.google.com/maps/dir//%EC%84%9C%EC%9A%B8%ED%8A%B9%EB%B3%84%EC%8B%9C+%EC%84%B1%EB%8F%99%EA%B5%AC+%EC%84%B1%EC%88%98%EC%9D%B4%EB%A1%9C+78/data=!4m6!4m5!1m1!4e2!1m2!1m1!1s0x357ca493e49ef401:0xc094aa7e17119038?sa=X&ved=1t:707&ictx=111)
    *   Korean: [서울 성동구 성수이로 78](https://map.naver.com/p/directions/-/14143836.261734452,4514889.223835517,%EC%84%B1%EC%88%98%EB%8F%99%EB%8C%80%EB%A6%BC%EC%B0%BD%EA%B3%A0%EA%B0%A4%EB%9F%AC%EB%A6%AC,37910590,PLACE_POI/-/car)
*   **Operating Hours:** Daily 11:00 - 22:00
*   **How to Get There from** KING STUDIO**:**
    *   **Public Transportation:** From Samseong Jungang Station (Line 9), transfer to Suin-Bundang Line at Seonjeongneung Station, alight at Seoul Forest Station, then a short walk. Approximately 40 minutes.

### TOP 2: Namsan Seoul Tower (남산 서울타워)

![](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2025/09/seoul_guide_trip.webp)

![](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2025/09/seoul_travel_namsan.webp)

![](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2025/09/seoul_travel_guide_namsan.webp)

*   **Why it's a Blink Must-Visit:** Who could forget the breathtaking drive and night views from **BLACKPINK**'s 'BOOMBAYAH' music video? Namsan Seoul Tower is one of Seoul's most iconic landmarks, offering panoramic views of the city, especially stunning at night. It’s a perfect place for Blinks to hang a love lock, capturing beautiful memories of their **K-Pop Trip** and enjoying the romantic atmosphere that even our idols experienced. Add this to your **BucketList** for an unforgettable **Korea Experience**.
*   **Address:**
    *   English: [105, Namsangongwon-gil, Yongsan-gu, Seoul, Republic of Korea](https://www.google.com/maps/dir//%EC%84%9C%EC%9A%B8%ED%8A%B9%EB%B3%84%EC%8B%9C+%EC%9A%A9%EC%82%B0%EA%B5%AC+%EB%82%A8%EC%82%B0%EA%B3%B5%EC%9B%90%EA%B8%B8+105/data=!4m6!4m5!1m1!4e2!1m2!1m1!1s0x357ca25654346d21:0xaa968cf4ff81a119?sa=X&ved=1t:707&ictx=111)
    *   Korean: [서울 용산구 남산공원길 105](https://map.naver.com/p/directions/-/14129189.8784071,4517750.8939209,%EB%A6%AC%ED%8B%80%ED%8C%8C%ED%8C%8C%ED%8F%AC%20%EC%97%B0%EB%82%A8%EC%A0%90,37430272,PLACE_POI/-/transit?c=14.00,0,0,0,dh)
*   **Operating Hours:**
    *   Mon-Fri 10:00 - 23:00
    *   Sat-Sun 10:00 - 24:00
*   **How to Get There from** KING STUDIO**:**
    *   **Public Transportation:** From Samseong Jungang Station (Line 9), transfer to Line 3 at Express Bus Terminal Station, alight at Dongguk University Station, then take a Namsan circulation bus or cable car. Approximately 50 minutes.

### TOP 1: Apgujeong Rodeo Street (압구정 로데오 거리)

![](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2025/09/seoul_travel_guide_kfood-1.webp)

![](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2025/09/seoul_travel_guide_seoul_cafe.webp)

![](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2025/09/seoul_travel_guide-1.webp)

*   **Why it's a Blink Must-Visit:** This is it, Blinks! The ultimate **BLACKPINK** hotspot. Apgujeong Rodeo Street has been a frequent haunt for the members since their debut, making it a true mecca for anyone seeking a genuine **BLACKPINK** **Korea Experience**. This vibrant street is a hub of fashion and gourmet dining, offering a glimpse into the trends and lifestyle favored by our idols. You might even hear exciting anecdotes or spot members here! It's an ideal place to discover hidden gems – from chic boutiques to exclusive restaurants – that our girls might frequent. Its close proximity to KING STUDIO makes it incredibly convenient for your **K-Pop Trip**.
*   **Address:**
    *   English: [Apgujeong-ro 50-gil, Gangnam-gu, Seoul, Republic of Korea](https://www.google.com/maps/place/%EC%84%9C%EC%9A%B8%ED%8A%B9%EB%B3%84%EC%8B%9C+%EA%B0%95%EB%82%A8%EA%B5%AC+%EC%95%95%EA%B5%AC%EC%A0%95%EB%A1%9C50%EA%B8%B8/data=!3m1!4b1!4m6!3m5!1s0x357ca387c74714b9:0x6098c57a71e0794c!8m2!3d37.5272835!4d127.0382217!16s%2Fg%2F119tfyf7c?entry=ttu&g_ep=EgoyMDI1MDkwOC4wIKXMDSoASAFQAw%3D%3D)
    *   Korean: [서울시 강남구 압구정로50길](https://map.naver.com/p/directions/-/14129189.8784071,4517750.8939209,%EB%A6%AC%ED%8B%80%ED%8C%8C%ED%8C%8C%ED%8F%AC%20%EC%97%B0%EB%82%A8%EC%A0%90,37430272,PLACE_POI/-/transit?c=14.00,0,0,0,dh)
*   **Operating Hours:** Varies by store
*   **How to Get There from** KING STUDIO**:**
    *   **Public Transportation:** A short walk of about 15 minutes from KING STUDIO, or a 5-minute taxi/bus ride. It's incredibly close!
*   **Recommended Spots:** This area is teeming with high-end restaurants and fashion boutiques that are known to be favorites of the **BLACKPINK** members. While I can't name specific establishments to protect their privacy, dedicated Blinks will love the thrill of discovering these hidden treasures. Look out for flagship stores of brands they've worn or chic French/Italian restaurants – your inner **K-POP Demon Hunter** will lead the way!
