For UK-based Jus2 fan Alison, a trip to Seoul wasn’t complete without stepping into the shoes of her favorite Jus2 subunit. At **KING STUDIO**, a professional K-Pop recording hub in Gangnam, she transformed from a casual listener to an artist interpreting Jus2’s sultry R&B masterpiece _FOCUS ON ME_—a track celebrated for its hypnotic blend of dark beats and sensual vocals.

The song’s lyrics, co-written by JB and Yugyeom, revolve around intense eye contact and mutual attraction, themes Alison connected with deeply. “_Focus on me / You and me_” became more than lyrics—they framed her 2-hour journey to embody the song’s magnetic energy under the guidance of KING STUDIO’s veteran producers.

## **Inside the KPop Experience: From Vocal Coaching to Studio Magic**

**1\. Pre-Recording: Decoding the "FOCUS ON ME" Vibe**

KING STUDIO’s vocal directors analyzed the track’s technical nuances with Alison:

*   **Breathy Vocals:** The song demands controlled, intimate delivery—a style perfected through mic techniques used by artists like Park Hyo-shin.
*   **Lyric Nuances:** Staff provided romanized Korean lyrics and translation sheets to help Alison articulate phrases like “_묘한 긴장감이 맴돌 때_” (When a strange tension lingers).
*   **Beat Sync:** Producers highlighted the track’s 90 BPM tempo, guiding Alison to match Yugyeom’s smooth ad-libs.

**2\. The Recording: Idol-Level Production**

The [**Diamond**](__GHOST_URL__/diamond/) **Package**  included:

*   **Studio A:** A soundproof booth with Neumann U87 microphones—the same used in EXO and Red Velvet sessions.
*   **Real-Time Mixing:** Engineers adjusted reverb to mirror the song’s atmospheric depth.
*   **Language Support:** English-speaking staff bridged communication gaps during coaching.

“Hearing my voice blend with JB’s harmonies was surreal,” Alison recalls. “It felt like a duet with my idols.”

*   **Mastering:** Balanced highs/mids to emulate the original track’s velvety finish.
