### Top 7 Seoul Travel Destinations for Hong Kong K-Pop Fans

Your ultimate Seoul travel guide beyond the recording studio

Starting from KING STUDIO in Gangnam, here are the seven must-visit destinations that Hong Kong female K-Pop fans consistently rank as their Seoul travel highlights:

1.  **K-Star Road (강남 한류스타거리)**

Distance from KING STUDIO: 5-minute walk  
Experience the iconic GangnamDols bear sculptures representing your favorite K-Pop groups, including BTS, EXO, and Girls' Generation. This colorful street art installation offers perfect Instagram opportunities while celebrating K-Pop's biggest stars.  
_Address: 407 Apgujeong-ro, Gangnam-gu, Seoul_  

2.  **Bukchon Hanok Village (북촌한옥마을)**

Distance from KING STUDIO: 25-minute subway ride  
Seoul's most Instagram-worthy traditional neighborhood features hundreds of preserved hanok houses offering stunning city views and authentic Korean architecture. Perfect for hanbok rental photos and cultural immersion.  
_Address: 37 Gyedong-gil, Jongno-gu, Seoul_

3.  **Hongdae District (홍대)**

Distance from KING STUDIO: 35-minute subway ride  
The vibrant heart of Seoul's youth culture, featuring K-Pop merchandise stores, themed cafes, street performances, and endless shopping opportunities. WithMuu and K-Pop Square offer extensive idol merchandise collections.  
Address: Hongik University area, Mapo-gu, Seoul

4.  **Myeongdong Shopping District (명동)**

Distance from KING STUDIO: 20-minute subway ride  
Seoul's premier shopping destination featuring K-beauty products, K-Pop merchandise stores, and the famous Myeongdong Underground Shopping Center. Perfect for cosmetics shopping and street food experiences.  
_Address: Myeongdong, Jung-gu, Seoul_

5.  **COEX Mall and Starfield Library (코엑스몰)**

Distance from KING STUDIO: 10-minute subway ride  
Home to the stunning Starfield Library, perfect for aesthetic photos, plus extensive shopping and the SMTOWN Museum celebrating SM Entertainment artists. The ultimate blend of culture and commerce.  
_Address: 513 Yeongdong-daero, Gangnam-gu, Seoul_

6.  **N Seoul Tower and Namsan Park (N서울타워)**

Distance from KING STUDIO: 30-minute trip  
Seoul's iconic landmark offers panoramic city views, romantic love lock bridges, and sunset photography opportunities. The cable car ride and mountain hiking provide perfect Seoul travel memories.  
_Address: 105 Namsangongwon-gil, Yongsan-gu, Seoul_

7.  **Insadong Cultural District (인사동)**

Distance from KING STUDIO: 25-minute subway ride  
Traditional cultural heart featuring tea houses, art galleries, hanbok rental shops, and authentic Korean craft stores. Perfect for cultural souvenirs and traditional Korean dining experiences.  
_Address: Insadong-gil, Jongno-gu, Seoul_
