### Top 7 BLACKPINK & ROSÉ Fan Destinations in Seoul

After completing her recording session, Jordyn had access to Seoul's incredible array of BLACKPINK-related attractions. Here are the [Top 7](__GHOST_URL__/ghost/#/editor/post/68c0c0083622ef3b1fc0f01b) destinations every BLINK should visit during their Seoul travel:

1.  **YG Entertainment Building + The SameE Cafe**  
    The iconic YG Entertainment headquarters in Hapjeong-dong is where BLACKPINK's magic begins. The SameE cafe directly across the street offers the perfect vantage point to spot your favorite idols while enjoying signature desserts and browsing official YG merchandise in the basement shop.
2.  **Seoul Tower & Banpo Bridge (Pink Lighting Events)**  
    Seoul's most iconic landmarks regularly transform with pink lighting to celebrate BLACKPINK events. Seoul Tower, Sebitseom Island, and Banpo Bridge create stunning pink-lit night views during major BLACKPINK promotions, offering unforgettable photo opportunities for BLINKs.
3.  **COEX Mall & Starfield Library**  
    This massive underground shopping complex features the Instagram-famous Starfield Library and numerous K-Pop merchandise stores. The area frequently hosts K-Pop events and offers easy access to entertainment company buildings in Gangnam.
4.  **Rakkojae Hanok Hotel (Bukchon Hanok Village)**  
    The traditional hanok hotel where BLACKPINK filmed their dreamy 2020 summer photoshoot. Located in Bukchon Hanok Village, this destination perfectly combines Korean traditional architecture with K-Pop culture.
5.  **Kyung Hee University Global Campus**  
    The stunning open-air amphitheater featured in BLACKPINK's "AS IF IT'S YOUR LAST" music video. This ancient Greek theater-inspired venue can accommodate 6,500 people and offers magnificent architecture perfect for BLINK pilgrimages.
6.  **Dosan Bunsik (Garosu-gil)**  
    The beloved local restaurant where Jisoo shared adorable Instagram photos. Famous for Korean-style snack foods and their signature tonkatsu sandwich, this spot offers authentic Korean dining experiences that BLACKPINK members love.
7.  **Ceramic Cafe JOOL**  
    The pottery workshop where Jisoo and ROSÉ created handmade ceramics during BLACKPINK's "24/365 with BLACKPINK" reality content. Visitors can participate in one-day pottery classes and create their own ceramic masterpieces.
