**STEP 4: Rehearsal & Key Adjustment**

This is where science meets artistry.

**The Key Finding Process:**

1️⃣ Initial Test Sing  
Jannah sang "Beautiful" in the original key while Lucia and Aiden listened carefully.

2️⃣ Professional Assessment

🎬 Take-by-Take Approach

Unlike karaoke (one full song attempt), professional recording captures sections: verse, pre-chorus, chorus, bridge. Each section gets multiple takes for optimal performance.

🎯 Real-Time Directing  
After each take, Lucia provided specific feedback:

*   "Beautiful breath control, but let's extend that last note by one beat."
*   "Perfect emotion! Now let's try one more with a bit more power."
*   "Your falsetto is gorgeous—use it more confidently in the bridge."

🎛️ **Live Monitoring by Aiden**  
While Lucia directed emotion, Aiden monitored technical quality:

*   Mic technique (distance consistency)
*   Pitch stability
*   Breath noise management
*   Dynamic range balance

🔄 The Compilation Process  
After 8-10 takes per section, Aiden and Lucia selected the best performances—sometimes even combining the emotion of Take 3 with the technique of Take 7. This is called "vocal comping," the same method used for actual K-Pop idol recordings.
