### Top 4 Jo & The Juice (Parnas Mall Branch) / 조앤더주스

![](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2026/01/-image----6772052.webp)

![](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2026/01/-image----2853496.webp)

![](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2026/01/-image----1050029.webp)

**The 'Behind' Story:**

If you are a true fan, you know Rosé’s obsession with this place. While it is a global chain, the Seoul branches are a holy grail for Blinks because Rosé famously declared her love for the "Iron Man" juice here. It is often cited as her go-to pick-me-up during busy schedules in Seoul. It's the easiest and most accessible way to start your Seoul Experience.

**Vibe & Menu:** The vibe is healthy, energetic, and modern.

*   **Rosé’s Pick**: You must order the "Iron Man" (Strawberry, Kiwi, Apple). It’s refreshing and exactly what she drinks to stay energized.

📍Address (Google) : [Yeouido-dong 22, Yeongdeungpo-gu, Seoul](https://maps.app.goo.gl/oB1Bg7DfnyRynais8)

### Top 3 Mimi Myeonga / 미미면가

![](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2026/01/-image----6219526.webp)

![](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2026/01/-image----4875836-2.webp)

![](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2026/01/-image----7017187.webp)

**The 'Behind' Story:**

Located in the trendy Sinsa-dong area, this spot is famous for being a "date spot" for Rosé and fellow member Jisoo. They have been spotted here enjoying a casual yet high-quality meal. It is a Michelin Bib Gourmand restaurant, meaning the food is certified delicious by experts, not just idols. It’s a must-add to your Korea Travel itinerary.

**Vibe & Menu:** A cozy, wooden-interior Japanese Soba noodle shop. It often has a line, so come early!

*   **Rosé’s Pick**: Uni (Sea Urchin) Soba or the Shrimp Tempura Soba. The broth is savory and deep—perfect for a light but satisfying meal.

📍Address (Google) : [29, Gangnam-daero 160-gil, Gangnam-gu, Seoul](https://maps.app.goo.gl/7sSGFeCCdz9cWroy6)  
📍Address (Naver) : [서울 강남구 강남대로160길 29](https://map.naver.com/p/directions/-/14139925.3074603,4511844.0790767,%EB%AF%B8%EB%AF%B8%EB%A9%B4%EA%B0%80%20%EA%B0%80%EB%A1%9C%EC%88%98%EA%B8%B8%EC%A0%90,21693586,PLACE_POI/-/transit?c=13.00,0,0,0,dh)  
✅Google Maps Tip: Search for "Mimi Myeonga".  
✅Solo-Dining: Possible at the counter seats.

### Top 2 Hannam Tatsu / 한남타츠

![](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2026/01/-image----5138989.webp)

![](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2026/01/-image----4798609.webp)

![](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2026/01/-image----137890.webp)

**The 'Behind' Story:**

For a more sophisticated evening, this is the spot. Rosé visited this high-end Izakaya (Japanese pub) with actress Kim Go-eun, showcasing her wide celebrity network. It represents the "Young & Rich" side of Seoul nightlife. If you want to see where the top stars unwind in private, this is a BucketList destination.

**Vibe & Menu:** Dim lighting, private atmosphere, and very upscale. It’s a place to dress up a little.

*   Rosé’s Pick: While specific orders vary, the Sashimi Platter and Futomaki here are legendary. Pair it with a highball or sake for the full experience.

📍Address (Google) : [68-15 Hannam-dong, Yongsan-gu, Seoul](https://maps.app.goo.gl/kC33GZqmTDMLBFY18)  
📍Address (Naver) : [서울 용산구 한남동 68-15](https://map.naver.com/p/directions/-/14138568.656826,4513944.7119649,%ED%95%9C%EB%82%A8%ED%83%80%EC%B8%A0,1233920777,PLACE_POI/-/transit?c=15.00,0,0,0,dh)  
✅Google Maps Tip: Search for "Hannam Tatsu"  
✅Solo-Dining: Difficult. Best for small groups (2-4 people). Reservations recommended.

### Top 1 Yeongcheon Yeonghwa / 영천영화

![](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2026/01/-image----9701710.webp)

![](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2026/01/-image----7215761.webp)

![](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2026/01/-image----4203501.webp)

**The 'Behind' Story:**

Taking the crown at #1 is the legendary beef restaurant, Yeongcheon Yeonghwa. Rosé and the Blackpink members are known meat lovers, and this spot is famous for having the best Hanwoo (Korean Beef) Tartare in the city. It is a staple in the K-Pop world; almost every major idol has signed an autograph here. Eating here is the quintessential ROSÉ favorite places Seoul experience.

**Vibe & Menu**: Bustling, authentic, and filled with walls covered in celebrity signatures.

*   **Rosé’s Pick**: You absolutely must order the Hanwoo Yukhoe (Beef Tartare) and the Galbi-sal (Grilled Beef Ribs). The quality of the meat melts in your mouth.

📍Address (Google) : [50-13 Cheongdam-dong, Gangnam-gu, Seoul](https://maps.app.goo.gl/25Kug2p8cAxisDQLA)  
📍Address (Naver) : [서울 강남구 청담동 50-13](https://map.naver.com/p/directions/-/14143088.6289023,4512392.5080831,%EC%98%81%EC%B2%9C%EC%98%81%ED%99%94%20%EC%B2%AD%EB%8B%B4%EC%A0%90,13124988,PLACE_POI/-/transit?c=15.00,0,0,0,dh)  
✅Google Maps Tip: Search for "Yeongcheon Yeonghwa Cheongdam".  
✅Solo-Dining: Possible, but Korean BBQ is traditionally a group activity.
