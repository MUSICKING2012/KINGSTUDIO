### 2\. Natasha’s “Never Let Go” Journey with Gold

Today’s star is Natasha, a 20-year-old ARMY from the US who flew to Seoul for **BTS FESTA**. She chose **Jung Kook – Never Let Go**, the heartfelt fan tribute from BTS’s 11th anniversary, inspired by its soaring melodies and emotive chorus.

**A. Meet the Team**

*   **Vocal Director:** Guides pitch, runs, and K-Pop stylistic nuances
*   **Manager:** Oversees session flow, mic setup, Q-box walkthrough
*   **Producer Aiden:** Engineers, arranges the instrumental, records your take

**B. Studio Tour & Mic Test**

Natasha’s experience began with a quick walk-through of our sound-proof booths, plus a mic test to dial in her tone. The manager showed her how to use the Q-box talkback system—exactly like at Korea’s top labels.

**C. Vocal Warm-Up & Coaching**

![](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2025/08/korea-travel-vocal-training-session-kpop-experience-king-studio-bts-jungkook-btsfesta.jpg)

Next, the vocal director led Natasha through K-Pop-style breath work and agility drills to nail Jung Kook’s iconic runs. Confidence soared as she mastered the pre-chorus harmonies.

**D. Rehearsal & Key Selection**

Before hitting record, Natasha rehearsed each verse and chorus. Through guided key adjustments, the director found her **sweet spot**, ensuring she felt effortless in Jung Kook’s range.

**E. Pro-Level Track Recording**

![](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2025/08/seoul-experience-song-key-adjustment-kpop-recording-king-studio-army-never-let-go.jpg)

Just like a BTS member, Natasha tracked verse by verse. The director gave real-time feedback on tone and emotion, while Aiden captured every nuance. In 90 minutes, she completed lead, backing vocals, and ad-libs.

**F. Rough Mix & Sweet-Spot Monitoring**

![](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2025/08/seoul-experience-final-monitoring-kpop-recording-king-studio-army-jungkook-gold-service-1.jpg)

With her raw takes done, Aiden crafted a quick rough mix. Natasha auditioned the balanced stems at the “sweet-spot”—our dedicated mixing seat—hearing her voice in perfect context.

**G. Welcome Kit & Keepsakes**

![](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2025/08/seoul-experience-final-monitoring-kpop-recording-king-studio-army-jungkook-gold-welcomket.jpg)

To celebrate her milestone, we gifted Natasha a KING STUDIO welcome kit: branded earphones, lyric booklet, and a custom session photo. A tangible memory of her Seoul adventure!
