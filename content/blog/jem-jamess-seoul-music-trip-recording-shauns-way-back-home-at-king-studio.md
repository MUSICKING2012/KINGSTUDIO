## 5 Seoul Spots That Capture the "Way Back Home" Vibe

We're going to be honest with you: SHAUN isn't an idol group with a pilgrimage map. He's a solo electronic-pop artist, and "Way Back Home" is a _mood_, not a location.

So instead of fake "SHAUN hangouts," here are 5 verifiable Seoul spots whose vibes genuinely match the song — late-night, nostalgic, electronic, the feeling of "going home" through a glowing city.

Counting down from **Top 5** to **Top 1**.

### Top 5 — Ikseon-dong Hanok Alley (익선동) | _Jongno-gu_

![](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2026/05/476506144_17962941890850340_896712343976704435_n.webp)

![](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2026/05/573856965_18533349010020568_9091180751723093786_n.webp)

![](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2026/05/359755866_1645320572640492_2265124073344216545_n.webp)

**Why it matches "Way Back Home":** The song is about returning to something familiar. Ikseon-dong literally _is_ return — a century-old hanok village transformed into modern cafés and bars without losing the old bones. Walking these narrow alleys at dusk feels like the song's bridge: nostalgic, intimate, soft-lit.

*   **Best time:** 5:00–7:00 PM (golden hour into early evening, lanterns turn on)
*   **Insider tip:** Skip the main alley and find the smaller side passages — that's where the real atmosphere lives
*   **Budget:** USD 8–20 (café or small dinner)
*   **Photo spot:** The arched lantern alley near Exit 6 of Jongno 3-ga Station
*   **From KING STUDIO (Seolleung Station, Gangnam-gu):** ~25 min by subway (Line 2 → Line 3 or Line 5)

📍Address (Google) : [Ikseon-dong, Jongno-gu, Seoul](https://maps.app.goo.gl/QznXtD5BhFMpyQf8A)  
📍Address (Naver) : [서울특별시 종로구 익선동](https://naver.me/F7F54gFk)

### Top 4 — Euljiro "Hipjiro" District (을지로) | _Jung-gu_

![](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2026/05/683789308_18326452762266640_5612367680263549547_n.webp)

![](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2026/05/541132703_17925936456104757_8408868695013794075_n-1.webp)

![](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2026/05/652798724_18102723362491239_635697824577309336_n.webp)

**Why it matches "Way Back Home":** This is the song's electronic underbelly. By day, Euljiro is industrial workshops; by night, hidden rooftop bars and synth-soaked listening rooms emerge above print shops. Retro-futuristic, neon-lit, the perfect aesthetic for the song's electronic edit.

*   **Best time:** 9:00 PM onwards
*   **Insider tip:** The best bars have no signage — look for unmarked elevators in old buildings
*   **Budget:** USD 15–35 (drinks + small plates)
*   **Photo spot:** Any rooftop with the old Seoul skyline behind you
*   **From KING STUDIO (Seolleung Station):** ~30 min by subway (Line 2)

📍Address (Google) : [26, Chungmuro 5-gil, Jung-gu, Seoul](https://maps.app.goo.gl/yx1qcdsgSNstQgEPA)  
📍Address (Naver) : [서울 중구 충무로5길 26](https://naver.me/IFgdHN1k)

### Top 3 — Seongsu-dong Café District (성수동) | _Seongdong-gu_

![](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2026/05/653642650_17967888152882856_4861317808712937435_n.webp)

![](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2026/05/629937526_18379422394155484_3169299748010244433_n.webp)

![](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2026/05/550222488_18422307160099610_4362501703293842740_n.webp)

**Why it matches "Way Back Home":** Seoul's "Brooklyn." Converted shoe factories now house design studios, vinyl bars, and electronic-music-friendly cafés. The industrial-meets-soft aesthetic is exactly the song's production palette: hard beats under tender vocals.

*   **Best time:** Late afternoon into night
*   **Insider tip:** Check out cafés with in-house DJ booths — many play exactly the kind of soft electronic that lives in "Way Back Home"
*   **Budget:** USD 10–25 (specialty café experience)
*   **Photo spot:** Brick warehouse façades along Yeonmujang-gil
*   **From KING STUDIO (Seolleung Station):** ~25 min by subway (Line 2 — direct)

📍Address (Google) : [78 Seongsu-ro, Seongdong-gu, Seoul](https://maps.app.goo.gl/LQxNv6Vrhs7VtMLo7)

### Top 2 — Haneul Park / Noeul Park (하늘공원·노을공원) | _Mapo-gu_

![](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2026/05/645431753_17888713419438296_6066553259220854211_n.webp)

![](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2026/05/494521711_17942197893004428_3695511796300404144_n.webp)

![](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2026/05/581026642_18374662594152713_4114876224624544346_n.webp)

**Why it matches "Way Back Home":** "Noeul" literally means _sunset_. This park exists for golden-hour melancholy. The pampas grass fields glow pink-gold as the sun drops behind the Han River — the exact emotional color of the song's chorus.

*   **Best time:** 1 hour before sunset (check seasonal times — earlier in winter)
*   **Insider tip:** Autumn (Oct–Nov) is the legendary pampas grass season, but spring cherry blossoms are equally cinematic
*   **Budget:** Free (bring a drink from a nearby convenience store)
*   **Photo spot:** Pampas grass field at peak golden hour
*   **From KING STUDIO (Seolleung Station):** ~40 min by subway + light walk uphill (Line 2 → Line 6)

📍Address (Google) : [95, Haneul Park-ro, Mapo-gu, Seoul](https://maps.app.goo.gl/aB6Vikxck2PLooWe9)  
📍Address (Naver) : [서울 마포구 하늘공원로 95](https://naver.me/5HkadHtS)

### Top 1 — Banpo Hangang Park & Moonlight Rainbow Fountain (반포한강공원) | _Seocho-gu_

![](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2026/05/654632731_18097109095795230_246742476947016501_n-1.webp)

![](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2026/05/656152216_18112977895739060_2955320699823872760_n-2.webp)

![](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2026/05/654053904_18100790306511138_6059041502814067212_n.webp)

**Why it matches "Way Back Home":** This is THE spot. "Way Back Home" is, at its heart, about going home along a river under city lights. Banpo Hangang Park gives you exactly that: the Han River flowing through Seoul, the Banpo Bridge's Moonlight Rainbow Fountain (the world's longest bridge fountain) lit up at night, locals on picnic mats, the city humming softly behind you. Press play on the Sam Feldt edit and you'll understand the song in a new way.

*   **Best time:** Fountain shows run evenings (typically 8:00–9:30 PM, seasonal — verify on the day)
*   **Insider tip:** Grab convenience-store ramyeon and beer, sit on the grass facing the bridge. That's the move.
*   **Budget:** USD 5–15 (picnic snacks)
*   **Photo spot:** Fountain mid-show with the city skyline behind
*   **From KING STUDIO (Seolleung Station):** ~20 min by taxi, ~30 min by subway (Line 9 → bus or walk)

📍Address (Google) : [115-5 Banpo-dong, Seocho-gu, Seoul](https://maps.app.goo.gl/hqxuP3yx4apWBtey8)
