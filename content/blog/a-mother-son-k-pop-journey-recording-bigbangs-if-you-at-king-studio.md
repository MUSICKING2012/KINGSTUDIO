In Seoul's Gangnam district, where K-Pop dreams take shape, **KING STUDIO** offers travelers an unparalleled opportunity to step into the shoes of their favorite artists. For Canadian mother-son duo **Yulan** and **Michael**, this became the defining moment of their Seoul trip when they recorded a heartfelt duet of BIGBANG's 2015 ballad _"If You"_ – a song etched in K-Pop history for its raw emotional depth and lyrical vulnerability.

## Why "If You" Resonates with Global Fans

Penned by **G-Dragon** during a period of personal reflection, _"If You"_ marked BIGBANG's first full-group vocal performance, abandoning their signature rap verses for a minimalist ballad structure. The track’s stripped-down arrangement – built on acoustic guitar and haunting harmonies – amplifies its lyrical themes of regret and lost love:

_"If it’s not too late / Can’t we get back together?"_  
– _"If You"_ (translated lyrics)

Critics praised its departure from conventional idol music, with _Billboard_ noting its "restrained, dreamlike hums" and "emotional authenticity rarely seen in mainstream K-Pop". For Yulan, who lived in Korea two decades ago, the song symbolized her enduring connection to Korean culture. "I’ve sung this at home for years," she shared, "but experiencing it professionally made me understand why it’s considered BIGBANG’s _saddest masterpiece_"
