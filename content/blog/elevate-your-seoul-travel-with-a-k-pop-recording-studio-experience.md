## Spotlight on Aviva & Max: “Dolly Parton – Jolene” DIAMOND Session

### STEP 1 Meet the Team

When siblings Aviva & Max arrived from the USA, they met:

![](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2025/08/korea-travel-song-parts-key-adjustment-kpop-recording-4.jpg)

*   **Jae Wook (Vocal Director):** personalized coaching to nail dynamics
*   **Jinny (Manager):** studio tour, mic positioning, and Q-box operation
*   **Aiden (Producer):** setting up recording chains and capturing that signature K-Pop polish

### STEP 2 Studio Tour & Mic Test

Sora led Aviva & Max through our sleek 60㎡ studio. They practiced with:

*   ✅ High-end Neumann U87 Ai microphones
*   ✅ Simple Q-box karaoke interface for warm-up tracks

![](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2025/08/seoul-trip-kpop-recording-experience-king-studio--2-.jpg)

### STEP 3 Vocal Training

![](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2025/08/kpop-experience-vocal-director-training-king-studio-seoul.jpg)

Jihyun introduced K-Pop vocal techniques—runs, riffing, breath control—so their voices could blend emotion and precision, vital for “Jolene”’s haunting plea.

### STEP 4 Rehearsal & Key Adjustment

Before hitting record, we split Dolly Parton’s parts between Aviva (lead verse) and Max (harmonies). Through rehearsal, we found each sibling’s ideal key for maximum comfort and impact.

### STEP 5 Pro-Level Recording

![](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2025/08/king-studio-vocal-director-kpop-recording-guidance-seoul.jpg)

Just like your favorite idols, Aviva & Max recorded in sections:

1.  Verse tracking
2.  Pre-chorus layering
3.  Chorus harmonies
4.  Final ad-libs

After 90 minutes, they reviewed takes with Aiden, corrected details, and re-recorded minor parts.

### STEP 6 Rough Mix & Monitoring

![](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2025/08/seoul-trip-final-monitoring-kpop-recording-diamond-service.jpg)

Aiden performed a quick rough mix. Together, they listened in the sweetest spot of our studio to finalize levels and tone. At wrap-up, we presented the siblings with a stylish KING STUDIO welcome kit—logo water bottle, tote bag, and lyric booklet.
