### Percy’s Diamond Service: DAY 2 – “TEN – STUNNER” Recording Workflow

**Client:** Percy (Germany)  
**Artist Cover:** TEN – STUNNER  
**Service Level:** [DIAMON](__GHOST_URL__/diamond/)

**1\. Arrival & Team Introduction**  
Percy meets our team:

*   **Vocal Director**: Expert in K-pop R&B/pop fusion techniques.
*   **Manager**: Guides the session flow and handles technical setup.
*   **Producer Aiden**: Oversees mix quality and final mastering.

**2\. Studio Tour & Mic Test**  
Manager escorts Percy through:

*   **Recording booth**: High-end condenser microphones and pop filters.
*   **Cue box tutorial**: Real-time monitoring controls and headphone mixing.
*   Quick **mic test** to ensure optimal levels.

**3\. Vocal Warm-Up & Training**

Under the vocal director’s guidance, Percy:

*   Practices **breath control** for TEN’s dynamic lines.
*   Learns **vocal techniques**: smooth runs, whisper-rap transitions, and tonal balance to emulate the **R&B-pop groove** in “STUNNER”.

**4\. Pre-Recording Rehearsal & Key Adjustment**

![](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2025/07/diamond-king-studio-key-adjustment-STUNNER-Korea-Travel.jpg)

*   Team reviews each song section (verse, pre-chorus, chorus).
*   Identify that TEN’s original key sits low for Percy.
*   Adjust instrumental to Percy’s optimal vocal range for comfortable delivery.

**5\. Professional Multi-Track Recording**

![](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2025/07/diamond-king-studio-part-directing-WayZenNi-Seoul-Trip.jpg)

*   Record each vocal part separately, as in idol workflows.
*   Director coaches phrasing: “Stunner, stunner, stunner you are” and “Fire, fire, fire” hooks.
*   Session lasts **1–1.5 hours**, capturing multiple takes for comping.

**6\. Rough Mixing & Sweet-Spot Monitoring**

![](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2025/07/diamond-king-studio-monitoring-revision-Kpop-experience-Seoul-Experience.jpg)
