## The DIAMOND Experience — Step by Step

  
The DIAMOND tier is the full idol-session walkthrough, not a quick karaoke booth. Here is how Alina's session ran, start to finish.

### Step 1 Meeting the team

Alina was introduced to the three people who'd run her session: Vocal Director Seung Won, Manager Jen, and Producer Aiden. Her dad, Johannes, sat in for the session — he'd booked it for her.

![Alina meeting vocal director Seung Won, manager Jen, and producer Aiden in the King Studio control room before her session](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2026/06/kingstudio-rose-apt-key-adjustment.webp)

### Step 2 The studio tour

Before any singing, a walk through the booth: the condenser microphone, pop filter, and the cue (headphone-monitor) system that lets you hear the backing track and your own voice balanced in real time — the setup ROSÉ herself would track on.

Walk-through of King Studio's vocal booth showing the condenser mic, pop filter, and cue system

### Step 3 Vocal warm-up & key check

![Alina warming up her voice with vocal director Seung Won ahead of recording](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2026/06/kingstudio-rose-apt-vocal-warmup.webp)

Seung Won took Alina through warm-ups and checked where her voice sat against APT.'s key. This matters more for this song than most.

### Step 4 Rehearsal & key adjustment — the hard part

  
Here's where Alina's session got real. The challenge with APT. is full-range usage: the verses sit in a tight, almost spoken low register, the chant is punchy and rhythmic, and the chorus jumps up and stays there. The song demands you move across your whole range and snap between attitudes — bratty chant, then big open belt — without the seams showing. Seung Won adjusted the key so the top of the chorus stayed powerful instead of straining, while keeping the low verses from disappearing.

Vocal director Seung Won adjusting the key as Alina rehearses APT.'s range

### Step 5 Full Rehearsal

![Alina running the full song in the booth, headphones on, during rehearsal](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2026/06/kingstudio-rose-apt-full-rehearsal.webp)

A full run in the booth, headphones on, to lock the arrangement before tracking for real.

### Step 6 Idol-style recording & directing

![Vocal director Seung Won coaching Alina line by line through APT.](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2026/06/kingstudio-rose-apt-directing.webp)

This is the DIAMOND difference. Rather than one nervous take of the whole song, parts are tracked separately and directed line by line — the chant, the verses, the chorus, the ad-libs — then comped together into one clean vocal, exactly how an idol record is built. Seung Won coached Alina through the sections that fought back.

Ruff Mixed played back in the control room — Alina heard her own APT. sounding like a finished record, and went home with the DIAMOND welcome kit.
