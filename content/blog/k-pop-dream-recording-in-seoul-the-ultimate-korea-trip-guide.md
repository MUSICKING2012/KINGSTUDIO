### Day 3 Spotlight: Percy’s “SEULGI – 28 Reasons” Diamond Session

German dancer **Percy** arrived for his fifth and final recording after two marathon days and four completed songs. Despite the fatigue, he chose Red Velvet star **SEULGI’s “28 Reasons”**—a track famous for its seductive _good-vs-evil_ storyline.

1.  **Meet the Team** – Manager greets Percy, then introduces Vocal Director Ji On and Producer Aiden. A quick chat aligns artistic goals and key pronunciation points (_K-팝 팬 경험 한국_).
2.  **Studio Tour & Mic Check** – Inside Studio A, Percy practices cue-box signals and tests Neumann mics while engineers set compressor thresholds for his baritone range.

![](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2025/07/Seoul-Kpop-Recording-Rehearsal-SEULGI-28-Reasons.jpg)

**3.Targeted Vocal Training** – Jisu isolates the breathy whisper of Seulgi’s verse and the chest-voice crescendos of the whistle-laced chorus, drilling vowel shape and diaphragm support.

**4.Key Selection & Rehearsal** – Vocal Director transposes the instrumental down a semitone after pitch analysis so that the infamous “_I kiss your brother_” line sits comfortably at F♯4.

**5.Part-by-Part Recording** – True to SM-style workflow, Percy tracks verse, pre-chorus and hook separately; punch-ins fix sibilants and sustain issues. After 90 minutes, comped takes are printed.

**6.Rough Mix & Sweet-Spot Review** – Real-time tuning, EQ and reverb create a radio-ready rough mix. Percy evaluates the balance in the control-room sweet spot and approves minor edits for final mastering.

Three days, five songs, one certified **Dream Come True**.

### Song Breakdown: Why “28 Reasons” Hits Different

SEULGI’s solo debut marries groovy bass, eerie whistles and string stabs to portray a lover who is “both angel and villain.” The _28_ symbolizes the 28 motivations of a toxic flirtation—and her age at release. Critics hailed the track’s Aaliyah-esque _R&B minimalism_ and its suspense-movie atmosphere.

**Key musical moments Percy mastered:**

*   Duality Concept – Switching from soft whisper to powerful belt mirrors the good/evil conflict.
*   Bass-Driven Hook – Heavy sub bass supports Seulgi’s staccato “28 reasons” refrain, demanding precise rhythmic control.
*   Final A Cappella Drop-Out – All instruments cut, exposing raw vocals—perfect for showcasing new technique acquired in the booth.
