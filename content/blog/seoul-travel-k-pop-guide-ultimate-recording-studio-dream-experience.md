### Today's Star: Yu Wing Hin's Musical Journey from Hong Kong

Today, we're thrilled to welcome Yu Wing Hin, a passionate K-pop fan from Hong Kong who chose Park Won's "All of My Life" for her K-Pop Making Class experience. This beautiful ballad, released in 2017, has captured hearts worldwide with its emotional depth and stunning vocal arrangements.

The song explores themes of love, personal growth, and finding meaning through relationships, making it a perfect choice for those wanting to showcase their vocal range and emotional expression.

**Step 1: Meeting the Dream Team**

Upon arriving at KING STUDIO, Yu Wing Hin was warmly welcomed by our professional team. Producer Aiden, with years of experience in the Korean music industry, and our dedicated studio manager began by explaining the day's journey ahead. The excitement was palpable as Yu Wing Hin realized she was about to experience the same process that creates the songs she loves so much.

"I've been a K-pop fan for over five years," Yu Wing Hin shared, "but I never imagined I'd actually get to experience what it's like to be in a real recording studio in Seoul!"

**Step 2: Studio Tour and Equipment Familiarization**

Our manager guided Yu Wing Hin through KING STUDIO's state-of-the-art facilities, explaining the function of each piece of equipment. The highlight was learning to use the cue box system - the same technology used by professional K-pop artists to hear themselves and the backing track while recording.

During the microphone test, Yu Wing Hin's eyes lit up as she heard her voice through the professional monitoring system for the first time. "It sounds so different from singing at home," she laughed, already feeling like a real K-pop trainee.

**Step 3: Understanding the K-Pop Industry with Producer Aiden**

Producer Aiden took time to explain the intricate world of K-pop production, covering the roles of A&R representatives, the studio creation process, and how Korean music companies develop their signature sounds. ### Top 7 K-Pop Destinations for Hong Kong Travelers

After your KING STUDIO experience, Seoul Travel offers countless opportunities to dive deeper into K-pop culture. Based on feedback from our Hong Kong visitors aged 20-35, here are the must-visit destinations:

**1\. Gangnam K-Star Road**

**Distance from** KING STUDIO**:** 15 minutes by subway **Google Maps:** [Gangnam K-Star Road](https://maps.google.com/search/Gangnam+K-Star+Road+Seoul) This iconic location features bear statues inspired by your favorite K-pop idols including BTS, EXO, and Girl's Generation, making it perfect for Instagram-worthy photos that will make your friends back home incredibly jealous.

**2\. HYBE Insight Museum**

**Distance from** KING STUDIO**:** 25 minutes by subway **Naver Map:** [https://map.naver.com/v5/entry/place/1944190916](https://map.naver.com/v5/entry/place/1944190916) This interactive museum showcases BTS and other HYBE artists' journey, offering an immersive experience that Hong Kong ARMY members absolutely love.

**3\. Hongdae Playground**

**Distance from** KING STUDIO**:** 20 minutes by subway **Google Maps:** [Hongdae Playground Seoul](https://maps.google.com/search/Hongdae+Playground+Seoul) The birthplace of Korean indie music and current hub for K-pop busking performances, perfect for discovering new artists and experiencing Seoul's vibrant youth culture.

**4\. Myeongdong Shopping District**

**Distance from** KING STUDIO**:** 18 minutes by subway **Google Maps:** [Myeongdong Seoul](https://maps.google.com/search/Myeongdong+Seoul) The ultimate destination for K-pop merchandise shopping, featuring countless stores selling official albums, lightsticks, and exclusive Korean cosmetics favored by idols.

**5\. Han River Park (Banpo Rainbow Bridge)**

**Distance from** KING STUDIO**:** 30 minutes by subway **Naver Map:** [https://map.naver.com/v5/entry/place/11491350](https://map.naver.com/v5/entry/place/11491350) A favorite spot featured in many K-pop music videos and variety shows, offering picturesque views perfect for capturing scenic photos reminiscent of your favorite idol's Instagram posts.

**6\. Itaewon Global Village**

**Distance from** KING STUDIO**:** 22 minutes by subway **Google Maps:** [Itaewon Seoul](https://maps.google.com/search/Itaewon+Seoul) International district with K-pop themed cafes and restaurants, plus the famous "Itaewon Class" filming locations that Hong Kong drama fans adore.

**7\. Lotte World Tower Seoul Sky**

**Distance from** KING STUDIO**:** 35 minutes by subway **Google Maps:** [Lotte World Tower Seoul](https://maps.google.com/search/Lotte+World+Tower+Seoul) Offering breathtaking panoramic views of Seoul, this iconic location appears in countless K-pop music videos and Korean dramas, making it essential for your Seoul Travel photography collection.
