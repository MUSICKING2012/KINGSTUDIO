Experience true K-Pop Recording in Seoul—where global fans become stars in just 2 hours!

1.  **Introduction to KING STUDIO’s K-Pop Making Class**

KING STUDIO brings the authentic idol recording experience to Seoul Travel itineraries. Our [K-Pop Making Class](__GHOST_URL__/k-pop-making-class/) invites global K-Pop lovers to step into a professional studio—complete with vocal director, manager, and producer—and record their favorite song in only two hours. From vocal warm-ups to rough mixes, every stage mirrors how top K-Pop hits are produced. Whether you’re a solo traveler or a family, our studio transforms your Seoul travel into a BucketList, Dream Come True moment.

2.  **Welcome Kent Family—From London to KING STUDIO**  
    Today’s stars are the Kent Family from London, BLINKs who chose “ROSÉ & Bruno Mars – APT.” for their cover.

3.  **Studio Tour & Mic Check**  
    Manager Ha Rim leads a quick tour of our hybrid analog-digital booths. At the cue box, he demonstrates headphone levels and mic technique—essential for that crisp K-Pop sound. A brief mic test sets the stage for the real deal.

4.  **Vocal Training with Ji On**  
    Vocal Director Ji On starts with warm-ups:

*   Breath control drills
*   Pitch slides to find each voice’s sweet spot
*   Rosé’s signature “Apateu, apateu…” chant to capture APT.’s playful mood

Kids giggle through the chant, while mom nails Rosé’s higher riffs and dad locks into Bruno’s groove.

5.  **Rehearsal & Key Adjustment**  
    Before hitting REC, Ji On explains each part:

![](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2025/07/seoul-trip-kpop-recording-key-adjustment-studio.jpg)

*   Chorus timing for the iconic call-and-response
*   Verse splits between Rosé and Bruno Mars lines

Through one rehearsal, they fine-tune the backing track’s key for every family member’s range.

6.  **Idol-Style Recording Session**  
    In true idol fashion, the session unfolds:

*   Part-by-part directing: Ji On guides emotional delivery on “Kissy face…”
*   Timing & pitch coaching: Aiden uses studio talkback for precision
*   Multiple takes: Capturing that perfect “APT.” energy

After 1–1.5 hours, they review the raw vocals and record retakes for any final tweaks.

7.  **Sweet Spot Mixing & Welcome Kit**

![](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2025/07/seoul-experience-final-monitoring-kpop-studio.jpg)

A quick rough mix places their vocals in the sweet spot. Monitoring through studio-grade monitors ensures balance. Finally, the Kent Family receives a KING STUDIO Welcome Kit—lyric sheet, and “APT.” cover.

8.  **Post-Studio Adventure: Seolleung & Jeongneung Royal Tombs**  
    Just a short subway ride to Seolleung Station, explore the serene Seolleung & Jeongneung Royal Tombs, a UNESCO World Heritage site in Gangnam. Wander peaceful forest paths and historic stone figures amid modern skyscrapers.
