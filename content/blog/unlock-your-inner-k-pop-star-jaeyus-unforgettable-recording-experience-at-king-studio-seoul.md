## A MIDZY's Dream Come True in Seoul

On a bright January afternoon in Gangnam, Seoul, 15-year-old JAEYU from Hong Kong stepped into KING STUDIO with excitement bubbling in her chest. As a dedicated MIDZY (ITZY's fan), she had dreamed of this moment for years - recording her favorite ITZY song "Gold" in the same professional environment where actual K-Pop stars create their music. This wasn't just another tourist activity; it was a carefully planned highlight of her Seoul travel adventure, a bucket list dream come true for a passionate K-Pop lover. JAEYU had researched various K-Pop experiences available in Seoul and discovered that KING STUDIO offered the authentic recording experience she craved, allowing her to step into the world of K-Pop production just like her ITZY idols.

What made JAEYU's visit particularly seamless was her impressive Korean language skills. Despite being from Hong Kong, she communicated fluently with the staff, eliminating any language barriers that might have hindered her experience. She had specifically chosen the DIAMOND package, which includes professional vocal training, recording, and post-production editing - essentially compressing the complete idol recording experience into a two-hour session.

## Inside KING STUDIO: The Professional K-Pop Environment.

Upon arrival at KING STUDIO in Gangnam, JAEYU was immediately struck by the professional atmosphere. The studio spans approximately 198 square meters, divided into STUDIO A and STUDIO B, with acoustic design optimized for vocal recording and instrumentation. The blend of high-quality analog equipment and cutting-edge digital systems creates an authentic recording environment that mirrors actual K-Pop production facilities.

"I can't believe I'm standing in the same recording space used by real K-Pop artists!" JAEYU exclaimed as she took in her surroundings. What makes KING STUDIO special is not just its state-of-the-art equipment but its professional staff. The studio's representative producer, Aiden, has been a sound engineer specializing in K-Pop since 2001 and has participated in approximately 400 albums over 22 years, working with legendary artists including Kangta, TVXQ, Jang Nara, DOK2, and Jessie.

KING STUDIO is the world's first K-Pop recording experience studio specifically designed for travelers, officially certified by the Korea Tourism Organization and Seoul City. Since 2016, it has welcomed K-Pop fans from around the world - Hong Kong, Singapore, the United States, and Europe - all seeking to create their own album as a special bucket list experience during their Korea trip.

## JAEYU's DIAMOND Experience: The Professional Recording Process

JAEYU's DIAMOND package experience began with an introduction to her personal vocal director who would guide her through the entire process. "If you can dream it, you can achieve it," the director told her, embodying the studio's philosophy. The package includes dedicated vocal training, recording and modification with pitch correction, mixing and mastering, instrumental accompaniment and lyrics in both Korean and romanized form, in-studio photos, and a special welcome kit.

The vocal director first helped JAEYU warm up her voice and provided tips on how to approach ITZY's energetic "Gold" track. They worked on breath control, pronunciation, and expression - the same process real idols go through before recording. JAEYU was fascinated to learn that each recording session begins with finding the vocalist's key and comfort range, ensuring the best possible performance

## The Final Product: Professional K-Pop Quality

While the complete mixing and mastering would take place after her visit (to be delivered within 2-3 weeks), JAEYU received a preliminary version of her recording to take home that day. The vocal director explained how the Diamond package includes professional editing that would perfect her pitch, timing, and add appropriate effects to make her cover sound as close to a professional K-Pop release as possible.

"I can't believe this is my voice!" she exclaimed in perfect Korean when hearing even the rough version of her recording. "It sounds so much better than when I sing along at home. The MIDZY in me is so happy right now!"

As part of her Diamond package, JAEYU also received a commemorative photo taken in the studio, a special welcome kit with KING STUDIO merchandise, and the promise of a professionally mastered MP3 file that would arrive in her email within weeks. The knowledge that her voice would undergo the same professional post-production process as her ITZY idols made the experience even more special.

## KING STUDIO offers several packages to suit different preferences:

1.  **K-Pop Making Class** (120 minutes, group experience): A group service with up to 6 participants focusing on the general K-Pop production process rather than individual recording.
2.  **Gold** (120 minutes): Freedom to choose any song (not limited to K-Pop), dedicated vocal director, original unedited audio files, in-studio photos, welcome kit, and interpretation in English, Chinese, or Japanese. Up to 3 people can participate in recording one song.
3.  **Diamond** (120 minutes) - JAEYU's choice: Everything in the Gold package plus professional editing including pitch correction, mixing, and mastering to achieve professional K-Pop quality results.
4.  **Premium** (180 minutes): The most comprehensive package including everything in the Diamond course plus full music video production for the complete idol experience.
