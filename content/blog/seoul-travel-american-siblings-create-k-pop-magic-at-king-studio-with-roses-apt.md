The song choice was perfect for these American visitors. The playful lyrics blend Korean culture with universal themes of fun and attraction, creating an accessible entry point for international fans.

Every professional recording begins with rehearsal, and Dorothy, Dan, and Kenneth's experience was no different. Given that "APT." originated from studio experimentation and playful energy, this rehearsal phase allowed the siblings to capture that same spontaneous joy while maintaining professional quality.

The beauty of "APT." lies in its accessibility – it's fundamentally about having fun and sharing good times, which perfectly matched the siblings' Seoul travel experience.
