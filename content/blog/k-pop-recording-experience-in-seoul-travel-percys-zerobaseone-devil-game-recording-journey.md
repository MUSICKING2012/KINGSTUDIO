### Percy's ZEROBASEONE "Devil Game" Diamond Experience: A German K-Pop Dancer's Recording Journey

**Understanding ZEROBASEONE: The Rising Global Phenomenon**  
ZEROBASEONE (제로베이스원), commonly known as ZB1, represents one of K-Pop's most exciting recent success stories. This nine-member multinational boy group emerged from Mnet's survival competition show "Boys Planet" and officially debuted on July 10, 2023, with their first mini album "YOUTH IN THE SHADE". The group consists of Sung Han Bin (leader and main dancer), Kim Ji Woong (visual and vocalist), Zhang Hao (main vocalist and center), Seok Matthew (Korean-Canadian member), Kim Tae Rae (main vocal), Ricky (Chinese member), Kim Gyu Vin (visual), Park Gun Wook (rapper and vocalist), and Han Yu Jin (visual and maknae).

The group's name symbolizes their journey from zero to one, representing their "glorious beginning" and commitment to sharing every step with their fans. Their official fandom name, ZEROSE (제로즈), cleverly plays on the concept that when you remove "ZB1" from "ZEROBASEONE," you get "ROSE," symbolizing the unity between the group and their fans. The fandom color is blue, and their official greeting is "D1, Be the ONE! Hello, we are ZEROBASEONE!"

ZEROBASEONE has achieved remarkable success since their debut, with their albums consistently topping charts worldwide. They made their Japanese debut in March 2024 with the single "YURA YURA" and continue to expand their global reach. The group is managed by WAKEONE Entertainment under a 2.5-year contract from their debut date.

**"Devil Game" Song Analysis: A Deep Dive into ZEROBASEONE's Latest Hit**

"Devil Game" serves as the second track from ZEROBASEONE's fifth mini album "BLUE PARADISE," released on February 24, 2025. This captivating dance-pop track showcases the group's versatility and artistic growth, featuring a mysterious and seductive concept that explores themes of irresistible attraction and temptation.

The song's musical composition, created by JayJay (MosPick), Park Woo Jeong, Young Chance, and JUNNY, features addictive synth bass themes with restrained, matte drumbeats that create a sexy yet mysterious groove. The track represents a modern interpretation of early 2000s pop music, filtered through ZEROBASEONE's unique artistic lens. The lyrics, written by Bang Hye Hyun, weave a narrative around "bad attraction that cannot be refused even though you know it's bad," using the metaphor of a "devil's hide and seek" mystery thriller concept.

The song opens with Seok Matthew's haunting introduction: "Ready or not, here I come / It's such a devil," immediately establishing the track's dark, alluring atmosphere. The Korean lyrics blend seamlessly with English phrases, creating lines like "섬찟한 속삭임 왠지 거부할 수 없어 no, I won't" (A chilling whisper, somehow I can't resist, no, I won't) and "그 함정 속에 빠져들어가" (Falling into that trap).

The chorus delivers the song's central message with powerful imagery: "Just like the movie, devil game, drama / 심장을 스친 강렬한 desire" (A fierce desire brushes past my heart). The track's bridge features an emotional climax with passionate solo vocals uniting for an exciting power note, though critics note that the final chorus could benefit from more dramatic amplification.

Music critics have praised "Devil Game" for its production quality, hooks, and longevity, with The Bias List giving it an impressive 8.75 out of 10 rating. The song's chunky bass, splashy synth, and nimble rhythm guitar create an instrumental foundation that hits all the right pleasure centers for funky K-Pop enthusiasts.
