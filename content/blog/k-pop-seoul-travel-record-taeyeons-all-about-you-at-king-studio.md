Today's featured artist, Linh from the United States, chose one of K-pop's most emotionally resonant ballads for her Seoul recording experience. TAEYEON's "All About You" (그대라는 시), originally featured as Part 3 of the Hotel Del Luna OST, represents the pinnacle of Korean ballad artistry. This haunting melody about eternal love and devotion provided the perfect canvas for Linh's **K-pop vocal recording session**.

**Step 4: Key Adjustment & Rehearsal**

  
Finding the perfect vocal range is crucial for authentic performance. Through careful rehearsal and key adjustment, we identified Linh's optimal vocal range for TAEYEON's emotionally complex ballad, ensuring comfortable delivery of both delicate verses and powerful choruses.

Our vocal director provided real-time guidance on emotional interpretation and technical execution, treating Linh exactly like a professional recording artist. Over 1-1.5 hours, we captured multiple takes to achieve the perfect blend of technical precision and emotional authenticity.

**Step 6: Mixing & Final Monitoring**

### TAEYEON & Girls' Generation Fan Destinations in Seoul

1.  **K-Star Road Gangnam** - Find Girls' Generation's official GangnamDol statue and capture photos at the iconic SNSD bear sculpture representing the "Nation's Girl Group".[](https://english.visitseoul.net/tours/k-star-road--where-stars-are-made_/15297)
2.  **Olympic Park Gymnastics Arena** - Visit the legendary venue where Girls' Generation held multiple historic concerts, including their record-breaking Phantasia Tour performances.[](https://www.8days.sg/hallyu-buzz/snsd-hold-4th-exclusive-concert-seoul-november-515611)
3.  **SM Entertainment Building (Apgujeong)** - The birthplace of Girls' Generation's career, where TAEYEON and the members trained and recorded their breakthrough hits.[](https://www.koreaherald.com/article/10344410)
4.  **SMTOWN COEX Artium** - Though permanently closed, this was the official SM Entertainment showcase space where fans could see Girls' Generation handprints and memorabilia.[](https://en.yna.co.kr/view/PYH20150113108500315)
5.  **Girls' Generation Forest (Yeouido Park)** - A memorial forest created by Taiwanese fans featuring dedicated trees for each SNSD member, located near Hyoyeon's individual memorial tree.[](https://www.trazy.com/spot/1322/girls-generation-forest-memorial-tree)
6.  **Hotel Seine Café (Ikseon-dong)** - Featured in Hotel Del Luna where TAEYEON's "All About You" served as the emotional soundtrack, this vintage-themed café perfectly captures the drama's nostalgic atmosphere.[](https://www.hallyusg.net/2019/07/24/filminglocations-hoteldelluna/)
7.  **Lotte Castle Premier (Samseong-dong)** - YoonA's residential area, offering fans a glimpse into where one of SNSD's most beloved members calls home.
