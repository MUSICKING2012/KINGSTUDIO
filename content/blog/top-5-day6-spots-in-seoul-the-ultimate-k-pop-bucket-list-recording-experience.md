### TOP 5: Cafe Onion Anguk / 카페 어니언 안국점

![](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2026/02/328803674_892506118870211_5840805062858749735_n.webp)

![](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2026/02/325977139_203599895488143_850479246691090720_n.webp)

![](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2026/02/328420630_513312050835431_4218001653435504350_n.webp)

**The 'Behind' Story**  
Why My Days flock here: This industrial-chic bakery café became legendary when Sungjin and Dowoon were spotted here multiple times during their off-days. The café's minimalist aesthetic and warm lighting made it a favorite for the members seeking quiet inspiration. Young K even mentioned in a V LIVE that the bakery's signature pastries remind him of pre-debut days when the band would treat themselves after grueling practice sessions.

**Vibe & Menu: What to Order**

*   **The Atmosphere**: Exposed brick walls, vintage furniture, and floor-to-ceiling windows—perfect for that moody, reflective DAY6 aesthetic.
*   **Must-Try**: Onion Croissant (어니언 크로와상) and Sea Salt Cream Latte—reportedly Sungjin's go-to combo.
*   **Fan Tip**: Arrive before 10 AM on weekdays to avoid the crowd and snag a window seat.

  
**Foreigner-Friendly Info**  
✅ Solo-dining friendly: Perfect for journaling or sketching while channeling your inner songwriter.  
🗺️ How to Find: Search "Cafe Onion Anguk" on Google Maps—it's a 5-minute walk from Anguk Station (Line 3), Exit 3.  
💬 English Menu: Available! Staff speaks basic English.  
💰 Budget: ₩8,000-₩15,000 (~$6-$11 USD)

📍Address (Google) : [Cafe Onion, 5, Gye-dong-gil, Jongno-gu, Seoul](https://maps.app.goo.gl/tUxADQviTbbTwk2u5)  
📍Address (Naver) : [서울 종로구 계동길 5 카페 어니언](https://map.naver.com/p/directions/-/14136075.2338156,4519905.947436,%EC%96%B4%EB%8B%88%EC%96%B8%20%EC%95%88%EA%B5%AD,1240190178,PLACE_POI/-/transit?c=15.00,0,0,0,dh)

### Top 4 Toriki-Joku  Hongdae / 토리키조쿠 홍대점

![](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2026/02/-image----9488642.webp)

![](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2026/02/-image----6216747.webp)

![](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2026/02/-image----2243945.webp)

**The 'Behind' Story**  
The late-night gathering spot: Toriki-Joku, a popular Japanese izakaya chain, holds a special place in DAY6 lore. Jae (eaJ) frequently mentioned this spot during his Twitch streams and ASC (After School Club) days, calling it his "comfort food zone" after long recording sessions. The members have been photographed near this location multiple times, and fans have reported casual sightings during late evenings.

**Vibe & Menu: What to Order**

*   **The Atmosphere**: Lively, casual izakaya with wooden interiors—think post-concert celebration vibes.
*   **Must-Try**: Karaage (Korean-style fried chicken) and Draft Beer—Jae's self-proclaimed favorite combo for "decompressing after a tough day."
*   **Group-Friendly**: Sharing platters and communal seating make this ideal for traveling with fellow My Days.  
    

Foreigner-Friendly Info  
✅ Great for groups: 2-6 people recommended.  
🗺️ How to Find: Search "Torimatsu Hongdae" on Google Maps—about 7 minutes from Hongdae Station (Line 2), Exit 9.  
💬 English Menu: Yes, with pictures!  
💰 Budget: ₩15,000-₩25,000 per person (~$11-$19 USD)  
⏰ Best Time: After 7 PM for the full izakaya experience.

📍Address (Google) : [2nd floor 102, Eoulmadang-ro, Mapo-gu, Seoul](https://maps.app.goo.gl/vqs5Et9Cvp5BKhTn9)  
📍Address (Naver) : [서울 마포구 어울마당로 102 2층](https://map.naver.com/p/directions/-/14128943.8623325,4516599.0072387,%ED%86%A0%EB%A6%AC%ED%82%A4%EC%A1%B0%EC%BF%A0%20%ED%99%8D%EB%8C%80%EB%B3%B8%EC%A0%90,1205779129,PLACE_POI/-/transit?c=15.00,0,0,0,dh)

### Top 3 Hangang River Park - Yeouido Area / 한강공원 여의도

![](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2026/02/SEVENTEEN-s-CARAT-Fan-Tour-Seoul-Hangang-River-TOP2_02-1.webp)

![](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2026/02/SEVENTEEN-s-CARAT-Fan-Tour-Seoul-Hangang-River-TOP2_01-1.webp)

![](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2026/02/SEVENTEEN-s-CARAT-Fan-Tour-Seoul-Hangang-River-TOP2_03-1.webp)

**The 'Behind' Story**  
Where inspiration meets tranquility: The Hangang River isn't just Seoul's scenic treasure—it's where DAY6 filmed key scenes for their iconic music video "You Were Beautiful" (예뻤어). The golden hour shots along the riverside cycling paths captured the bittersweet nostalgia that defines DAY6's sound. Young K has mentioned in interviews that walking along the Hangang helps him clear his mind and write lyrics.

**Vibe & Experience: What to Do**

*   **The Atmosphere**: Expansive riverside views, bicycle rentals, and portable food stalls (떡볶이, ramyeon, chimaek).
*   **Must-Do**: Rent a bike and ride along the cycling path during sunset—recreate the "You Were Beautiful" MV aesthetic for your Instagram.
*   **Fan Ritual**: Bring a portable speaker, play DAY6's discography, and have a solo picnic during golden hour.

**Foreigner-Friendly Info**  
✅ Solo OR group-friendly: Both work beautifully here.  
🗺️ How to Find: Search "Yeouido Hangang Park" on Google Maps—take Line 5 to Yeouinaru Station, Exit 3.  
💬 Bike Rental: Available with passport; staff speaks basic English.  
💰 Budget: ₩3,000-₩5,000 for bike rental (~$2-$4 USD); street food ₩5,000-₩10,000.  
📸 Photo Op: Best lighting: 5:30-7 PM (Spring/Summer), 4-5:30 PM (Fall/Winter).

📍Address (Google) : [Yeouido-dong 8, Yeongdeungpo-gu, Seoul](https://maps.app.goo.gl/96TaFhPdfhsTS92WA)  
📍Address (Naver) : [서울 영등포구 여의도동 8](https://map.naver.com/p/directions/-/14130320.2833083,4512725.5880879,%EC%97%AC%EC%9D%98%EB%8F%84%20%ED%95%9C%EA%B0%95%EA%B3%B5%EC%9B%90,13416869,PLACE_POI/-/transit?c=15.00,0,0,0,dh)

The 'Behind' Story  
The legendary writing sanctuary: Cafe Necessary was immortalized by Young K himself, who revealed in multiple interviews and V LIVEs that he wrote significant portions of DAY6's lyrics here. The café's quiet corners and aromatic coffee fueled countless songwriting sessions. Though the original Hongdae location has closed, My Days worldwide still reference this spot as the birthplace of tracks that defined DAY6's discography.

The Legacy Lives On  
While you can't visit the exact original location, the spirit of "Cafe Necessary" represents something bigger: the countless independent Seoul cafés where K-Pop artists find creative refuge. Young K's story reminds us that K-Pop magic happens in everyday spaces—which leads us to your own creative moment...
