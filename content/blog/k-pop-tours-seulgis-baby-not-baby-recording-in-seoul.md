Percy's DIAMOND Session: Covering SEULGI’s “Baby, Not Baby”  
Today marks Day 3 of Percy’s Seoul Trip, and he’s about to begin his 4th recording. After two days of completing one song each, Percy—exhausted yet laser-focused—faces his final challenge: SEULGI’s title track “Baby, Not Baby” from her mini-album Accidentally On Purpose (2025).

Lyrical breakdown and pronunciation of Korean and romanized verses. The backing track’s key is fine-tuned to Percy’s range, ensuring comfort without sacrificing the song’s dramatic energy.

### 3.Song Spotlight: SEULGI’s “Baby, Not Baby”

SEULGI (born Feb 10, 1994) trained seven years at SM Entertainment before debuting with Red Velvet in 2014. Known for her versatile vocals (G♯3 to B♭4) and “gap moe” contrast between cute and charismatic, she overcame early vocal challenges through rigorous training.

“Baby, Not Baby” blends funk bass, electric guitar, and orchestral flourishes into a dramatic pop-dance anthem. The lyrics—“I’m your baby, not baby”—challenge listeners to see beyond surface labels and embrace complexity. Its playful yet empowering tone encourages self-identity and defying expectations.
