### Ruike's Journey: Recording JOY's "Hello" Step by Step

Ruike's Personal Vocal Gold experience began weeks before she even stepped foot in KING STUDIO. After booking her session online, she received a comprehensive pre-session survey designed to understand her musical background, vocal goals, and favorite songs. This preliminary information allows KING STUDIO's vocal directors to prepare personalized recommendations before the actual session.

**STEP 1:** [**Pre-Session Demo Submission**](https://forms.gle/jzUtDzi7BCV9o5xK8)

Seven days before her scheduled session, Ruike recorded a demo of herself singing one of her favorite songs at home in Singapore. She didn't need professional equipment — a smartphone recording was perfectly sufficient. This demo served as crucial diagnostic material, allowing the vocal director to analyze her natural vocal qualities, identify her comfortable range, assess her vocal habits, and begin formulating song recommendations that would suit her voice.

The pre-submission process ensures that when Ruike arrived at KING STUDIO, her vocal director had already invested time studying her voice. This preparation maximizes the value of the two-hour in-person session, eliminating the need for lengthy initial assessments.​

**STEP 2: Professional Song Recommendation**

Based on Ruike's demo, KING STUDIO's vocal director carefully selected several song options that matched her vocal characteristics. Among these recommendations was JOY's "Hello," a modern rock remake of the classic ballad that perfectly suited Ruike's vocal color and range. "Hello" features JOY's signature dreamy, crystal-clear vocal lines over a refreshingly nostalgic soundscape, blending hopeful lyrics with contemporary production.​

The song choice wasn't random — it was strategically selected based on Ruike's vocal timbre, comfortable pitch range, emotional expressiveness, and technical capabilities. The vocal director provided detailed explanations for why "Hello" would allow Ruike to showcase her natural strengths while also challenging her to grow as a vocalist.

**STEP 3: Final Song Selection and Session Day Arrival**

![](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2025/10/Seoul-Travel-Kpop-experience-kpop-Recording-KingStudio-1.webp)

After reviewing the recommendations, Ruike and her vocal director collaboratively confirmed "Hello" as the final selection. This collaborative approach ensures that clients feel personally invested in their song choice rather than simply following instructions. On the day of her session, Ruike arrived at KING STUDIO's professional recording facility, located conveniently in Seoul's vibrant Gangnam district.​

**STEP 4: Meeting the KING STUDIO Team**

![](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2025/10/seoul-experience-vocal-director-consultation-kpop-recording-1.webp)

Upon arrival, Ruike was introduced to the three-person team who would guide her through the experience. The vocal director, responsible for analyzing her voice and providing training; the manager, serving as interpreter and session coordinator to ensure smooth communication; and producer Aiden, the sound engineer who would handle all technical aspects of the recording.

**STEP 5: Consultation and Goal Setting**

Before beginning any training or recording, the team spent quality time in conversation with Ruike, exploring what she hoped to achieve through the Personal Vocal service. Did she want to understand her vocal strengths? Was she curious about proper vocal technique? Did she simply want to experience what it feels like to record in a professional K-Pop studio? This dialogue ensured that every subsequent step of the session aligned with Ruike's personal goals.​

**STEP 6: Studio Tour and Equipment Introduction**

![](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2025/10/Kpop-experience-kpop-Recording-KingStudio-1.webp)

The manager guided Ruike through KING STUDIO's professional recording booth, explaining the equipment and demonstrating how to use the cue box system that allows communication between the recording booth and control room. A brief microphone test helped Ruike become comfortable with the professional setup. This orientation phase reduces nervousness and helps clients feel at ease in what might otherwise be an intimidating environment.​

**STEP 7: "Before" Recording**

Before any formal training began, Ruike recorded a "before" version of "Hello." This baseline recording serves multiple purposes — it provides a reference point for measuring improvement, helps the vocal director identify specific areas for focused training, and gives Ruike herself a tangible comparison to appreciate her progress by the end of the session.

**STEP 8: Comprehensive Vocal Analysis and Training (40 minutes)**

This segment represents the heart of KING STUDIO's Personal Vocal Gold service. The vocal director began with simple vocal warm-ups, including basic scale exercises that revealed Ruike's fundamental vocal range, initial impressions of her tone quality, comfortable singing zones (tessitura), and natural resonance patterns.

Following the warm-up, the director conducted diagnostic vocal exercises designed to comprehensively evaluate Ruike's voice. Lip trills and specific vowel-based exercises revealed detailed information about her vocal weight and texture, specific timbral characteristics (brightness, warmth, breathiness), register transitions between chest voice and head voice, breath support and control, and vocal agility and flexibility.​

Armed with this comprehensive understanding, the vocal director spent the remainder of the 40-minute segment providing targeted training specifically tailored to "Hello." Ruike learned techniques for maintaining breath support during sustained notes, achieving the dreamy, flowing quality characteristic of JOY's vocal style, navigating the song's emotional arc convincingly, and executing technical passages with clarity and confidence.

Throughout this training, the director continuously adjusted the approach based on Ruike's responses, ensuring that every minute was spent addressing her specific needs rather than following a generic curriculum.​

**STEP 9: Performance Training and Stage Presence (15 minutes)**

![](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2025/10/kpop-vocal-training-analysis-seoul-guide-travel-kingstudio.webp)

After a brief five-minute rest, the session shifted focus to performance skills. While the previous segment concentrated on vocal technique, this phase addressed the reality that singing isn't just about sound — it's also about presence and connection. The vocal director coached Ruike on proper microphone handling techniques used by professional performers, strategies for connecting emotionally with an audience even in an intimate setting, physical posture and breathing coordination while performing, and methods for conveying the emotional content of "Hello" through subtle performance choices.

This segment acknowledges that many KING STUDIO clients aren't just training for recording booths — they want to feel confident performing in front of friends, at karaoke gatherings, or even at amateur showcases. The performance training bridges the gap between technical skill and real-world application.​

**STEP 10: Professional K-Pop Style Recording Session (20 minutes)**

![](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2025/10/korea-travel-kpop-recording-studio-experience.webp)

Now came the moment Ruike had been anticipating — the actual recording session. Using the exact same production methods employed for professional K-Pop releases, the team guided Ruike through recording the first verse of "Hello." Producer Aiden monitored technical quality from the control room while the vocal director provided real-time directional feedback through the cue box system.

K-Pop recordings are famously meticulous, often requiring multiple takes of individual phrases to capture the perfect emotional tone and technical execution. Ruike experienced this firsthand, recording certain passages several times while the director coached her on subtle adjustments — a slightly different vowel shape here, a more supported breath there, a gentler onset on a particular note.

This authentic K-Pop recording experience differs dramatically from typical tourist activities. Ruike wasn't simply singing along to a backing track in a karaoke booth — she was actively collaborating with professionals to create a polished, studio-quality recording, making artistic choices and receiving expert guidance at every step.​

**STEP 11: Comprehensive Feedback and Future Guidance (15 minutes)**

![](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2025/10/seoul-experience-detailed-vocal-directing.webp)

Following the recording, the vocal director sat down with Ruike for an in-depth feedback session. This conversation included a detailed explanation of Ruike's "Vocal Fingerprint" — her unique combination of strengths, areas for improvement, distinctive tonal characteristics, habitual vocal patterns, and natural expressiveness.

The director answered all of Ruike's questions about the process, providing insights into how professional K-Pop vocalists train and prepare for recordings. Most importantly, the director outlined a practical roadmap for Ruike's continued vocal development after returning to Singapore, including specific exercises to practice, song recommendations for different practice goals, and guidance on how to maintain vocal health while improving technique.​

**STEP 12: Personalized Vocal Analysis Report Delivery**

Within one week of her session, Ruike received a comprehensive PDF report containing all the insights from her Personal Vocal Gold experience. This detailed document included measured vocal range and comfortable singing zone, specific descriptions of her tonal characteristics, evaluation of her vocal techniques (breath support, resonance, register transitions), identification of current strengths and areas for growth, and most valuably, customized song recommendations in three categories.

"Perfect Match" songs that ideally suit her current vocal characteristics, "Growth Catalyst" songs that will help her develop specific technical skills, and "Exploration" songs in styles she hasn't tried but might enjoy exploring. The report also featured references to artists with similar vocal qualities, providing models for Ruike to study and learn from.​

**STEP 13: Final Monitoring and Welcome Kit**

Before leaving KING STUDIO, Ruike listened to a rough mix of her recording at the studio's "sweet spot" — the acoustically optimal listening position where the room's sound is most accurate. Hearing her voice through professional monitors in a properly treated space gave her a realistic sense of her vocal quality. She also received KING STUDIO's welcome kit, a memento of her experience and a tangible reminder of her Seoul travel adventure.​
