### Explore Seoul Like K-Pop Demon Hunters: Top 7 Must-Visit Locations

Your **Seoul Travel** experience isn't complete without visiting the iconic locations featured in Netflix's hit anime "K-Pop Demon Hunters"! When K-pop superstars Rumi, Mira and Zoey aren't selling out stadiums, they're using their secret powers to protect their fans from supernatural threats—and now you can follow in their animated footsteps through real Seoul locations.

Here are the top 7 K-Pop Demon Hunters filming locations to visit during your Seoul adventure, starting from KING STUDIO:

**1\. COEX K-POP Square (강남구)**

**Distance from KING STUDIO: 15 minutes by subway** After leaving exit 5 or 6 from Samseong Station (Line 2), you'll find Korea's largest curved LED billboard. This stunning digital display features prominently in the anime and is a must-see for any K-Pop fan.

*   **Address:** 513 Yeongdong-daero, Gangnam-gu, Seoul
*   **Google Maps:** [COEX Mall](https://maps.google.com/maps?q=COEX+Mall+Seoul)
*   **Naver Maps:** [코엑스몰](https://map.naver.com/v5/search/%EC%BD%94%EC%97%91%EC%8A%A4%EB%AA%B0)

**2\. N Seoul Tower (남산타워)**

**Distance from KING STUDIO: 25 minutes by taxi** The iconic tower serves as a crucial backdrop in K-Pop Demon Hunters, representing Seoul's skyline in the animated series.

*   **Address:** 105 Namsangongwon-gil, Yongsan-gu, Seoul
*   **Google Maps:** [N Seoul Tower](https://maps.google.com/maps?q=N+Seoul+Tower)
*   **Naver Maps:** [N서울타워](https://map.naver.com/v5/search/N%EC%84%9C%EC%9A%B8%ED%83%80%EC%9B%8C)

**3\. Bukchon Hanok Village (북촌한옥마을)**

**Distance from KING STUDIO: 30 minutes by subway** Tucked between two of Seoul's most popular palaces, Bukchon Hanok Village is a neighborhood filled with traditional Korean houses known as hanok. The anime beautifully captures this historic neighborhood's charm.

*   **Address:** 37 Gyedong-gil, Jongno-gu, Seoul
*   **Google Maps:** [Bukchon Hanok Village](https://maps.google.com/maps?q=Bukchon+Hanok+Village+Seoul)
*   **Naver Maps:** [북촌한옥마을](https://map.naver.com/v5/search/%EB%B6%81%EC%B4%8C%ED%95%9C%EC%98%A5%EB%A7%88%EC%9D%84)

**4\. Naksan Park (낙산공원)**

**Distance from KING STUDIO: 35 minutes by subway** Featured prominently in several anime scenes, this hilltop park offers stunning city views and appears in memorable K-Pop Demon Hunters moments.

*   **Address:** 41 Naksan-gil, Jongno-gu, Seoul
*   **Google Maps:** [Naksan Park](https://maps.google.com/maps?q=Naksan+Park+Seoul)
*   **Naver Maps:** [낙산공원](https://map.naver.com/v5/search/%EB%82%99%EC%82%B0%EA%B3%B5%EC%9B%90)

**5\. Seoul Hanbang Promotion Center (서울한방진흥센터)**

**Distance from KING STUDIO: 20 minutes by subway** Since the airing of K-POP Demon Hunters, visitor numbers at the Seoul Hanbang Promotion Center have nearly tripled, making it a trending Seoul travel destination for anime fans.

*   **Address:** 64 Jongno 47-gil, Jongno-gu, Seoul
*   **Google Maps:** [Seoul Hanbang Promotion Center](https://maps.google.com/maps?q=Seoul+Hanbang+Promotion+Center)
*   **Naver Maps:** [서울한방진흥센터](https://map.naver.com/v5/search/%EC%84%9C%EC%9A%B8%ED%95%9C%EB%B0%A9%EC%A7%84%ED%9D%A5%EC%84%BC%ED%84%B0)

**6\. Dongdaemun Design Plaza (DDP)**

**Distance from KING STUDIO: 25 minutes by subway** The futuristic architecture of DDP appears in several anime scenes, representing Seoul's modern innovation spirit.

*   **Address:** 281 Eulji-ro, Jung-gu, Seoul
*   **Google Maps:** [Dongdaemun Design Plaza](https://maps.google.com/maps?q=Dongdaemun+Design+Plaza+Seoul)
*   **Naver Maps:** [동대문디자인플라자](https://map.naver.com/v5/search/%EB%8F%99%EB%8C%80%EB%AC%B8%EB%94%94%EC%9E%90%EC%9D%B8%ED%94%8C%EB%9D%BC%EC%9E%90)

**7\. Hongdae Street (홍대거리)**

**Distance from KING STUDIO: 40 minutes by subway** The vibrant nightlife and street performances of Hongdae capture the energetic spirit showcased throughout the anime series.

*   **Address:** Hongik-ro, Mapo-gu, Seoul (Hongik University area)
*   **Google Maps:** [Hongdae Street](https://maps.google.com/maps?q=Hongdae+Street+Seoul)
*   **Naver Maps:** [홍대거리](https://map.naver.com/v5/search/%ED%99%8D%EB%8C%80%EA%B1%B0%EB%A6%AC)
