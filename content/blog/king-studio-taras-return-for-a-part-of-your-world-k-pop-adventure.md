### Deep Dive: “Part of Your World” – Why It Works in a K-Pop Booth

### **Musical DNA**

*   **Key & Mood:** Originally in F major, giving the ballad a bright yet wistful color.
*   **I-Want Structure:** Ashman insisted on a Broadway “I Want” number to frame Ariel’s longing. K-Pop thrives on relatable yearning, making it easy to localize the emotion.
*   **Dynamic Range:** Soft storytelling in verses vs. belted chorus—perfect for showcasing vocal agility that K-Pop fans crave.

### KING STUDIO’s 120-Minute Workflow (DIAMOND Tier)

1.  **Tour & Briefing (0-05 min):** Gear overview, song key confirmation.

2.  **Warm-Ups (05-15 min):** Breath, lip trills, range mapping.

3.  **Guide Track & Pilot Take (15-20 min):** Establish phrasing, mark trouble spots.

4.  **Main Takes (20-100 min):** 3-5 full runs plus spot punches.

5.  **Ruff Mix (100-110 min):** EQ, light compression, reverb plate.
