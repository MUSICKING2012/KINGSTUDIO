### Why Kimberly chose "Love wins all"

Kimberly didn't pick a high-energy title-track flex. She picked the quietest, most exposed song IU has put out in years.

"Love wins all" is IU's January 2024 pre-release single — a soft piano ballad from the EP The Winning, with a now-famous music video starring BTS's V. It is, technically, one of the hardest kinds of songs to sing: there's nowhere to hide. No big beat to carry you, no harmonies to lean on. Just breath, pitch, and restraint. For a confident singer that's a challenge. For someone who hadn't really sung in a long time, it's a deep end.

And that's the part that makes Kimberly's choice matter. She's been a UAENA for years — IU is her artist, "Love wins all" is the song she loves singing most — so when she wanted to give herself something for a special day, this was the one. Not because it was easy, but because it was hers.

She was honest about the nerves. She told us she hadn't sung a song all the way through, let alone recorded one, in a long time. Her voice felt tight to her, a little stuffy, out of shape from disuse. She came in genuinely unsure whether her voice would hold up under a microphone — which is a very different thing from belting in the shower, where no one is listening back. That gap, between "I sing for myself" and "I'm about to hear myself recorded," is the real challenge point of her session, and it's why what happened in the booth landed the way it did.

If you're a fan abroad sitting on a song you love but afraid you're "not a good enough singer" to record it — Kimberly is the case study. You don't book this because you're already great. You book it to find out what your voice can actually do.

### Inside the booth: what the PREMIUM session actually looked like

### STEP 2 The studio and the gear

A real recording booth, real monitoring setup — the same kind of environment a K-pop artist tracks in. The point of the PREMIUM tier isn't a karaoke room with better speakers; it's that the space and the equipment are the genuine article, so the recording you take home actually sounds like a record.

### STEP 3 Vocal training and warm-up

![Kimberly in vocal training with KING STUDIO vocal director Jisun before recording](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2026/06/kimberly-vocal-training-director-jisun.webp)

Before any recording, Jisun ran Kimberly through vocal training to loosen up the voice she'd described as "stuffy." This is where the rust starts coming off — opening up the breath, finding where the voice sits, getting it moving again after a long time unused.

### STEP 4 Key check and rehearsal

Adjusting the backing-track key to fit Kimberly's voice for "Love wins all"

Then the practical engineering: adjusting the backing track's key so it fit her voice rather than forcing her to chase IU's original. "Love wins all" lives or dies on the soft, sustained phrases, so the rehearsal focused there — getting the breath control steady and the pitch landing cleanly on the quiet parts, the exact spots that expose a tired voice.

### STEP 5 Idol-style recording

![Vocal director Jisun directing Kimberly through her takes in the recording booth](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2026/06/kimberly-recording-booth-love-wins-all.webp)

Then the real thing, tracked the way idols do it — recording in sections and across multiple takes, comped together into one clean vocal rather than demanding a flawless single run-through. This is the difference between "record yourself" and being produced: you're allowed to be human, and the best of you gets assembled into the final.

### STEP 6 Directing through the hard parts

![Kimberly listening back to her finished, monitored recording at KING STUDIO](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2026/06/kimberly-vocal-directing-session.webp)

This is where Jisun earned the gratitude Kimberly kept expressing. A vocal director doesn't just hit record — she shapes the take in real time: where to breathe, where to pull back, where to let a phrase open up. Guided start to finish, hands-on, one detail at a time. Kimberly said working with a director like that, for the first time in her life, was on its own an enormous help — and she thanked Jisun specifically and warmly for it.

### STEP 7 Final monitoring — and the MV

![Kimberly sharing her review of the KING STUDIO PREMIUM experience](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2026/06/kimberly-final-recording-monitoring.webp)

Last, she listened back to the finished, monitored recording.
