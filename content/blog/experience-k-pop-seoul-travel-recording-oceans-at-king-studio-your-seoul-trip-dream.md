### The Song Choice: Understanding Hillsong UNITED's "Oceans (Where Feet May Fail)"

For her DIAMOND session, Amber selected Oceans (Where Feet May Fail)﻿, a powerful contemporary worship anthem by Australian worship collective Hillsong UNITED. This choice reveals something profound about the breadth of K-pop recording at KING STUDIO – it's not limited to K-pop songs alone; it's about the K-pop production system.

**About "Oceans (Where Feet May Fail)":**

Released on Hillsong UNITED's 2013 studio album _Zion_, "Oceans (Where Feet May Fail)" became the band's most iconic track. The song draws inspiration from the biblical account of Peter walking on water (Matthew 14:22-31), where Peter steps out of the boat toward Jesus but begins to sink when doubt overwhelms him.

The lyrics capture this spiritual narrative:

> _You call me out upon the waters / The great unknown where feet may fail / And there I find You in the mystery / In oceans deep, my faith will stand_

The song's bridge is particularly powerful:

> _Spirit lead me where my trust is without borders / Let me walk upon the waters / Wherever You would call me / Take me deeper than my feet could ever wander / And my faith will be made stronger_

This composition required Amber to deliver powerful, emotionally resonant vocals with dynamic range – exactly the skill set K-pop vocal directors train artists to perfect. The song's spiritual depth combined with its vocal demands made it an ideal choice for a professional K-pop recording session.

### Amber's DIAMOND Session: Step-by-Step Inside the K-Pop Recording Process

**Step 1: The Studio Introduction & Professional Team**

![K-pop Recording Experience - Vocal Part Assignment and Key Adjustment at Seoul Guide Travel King Studio](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2025/11/King-Studio-Seoul-Kpop-Vocal-Part-Setup-Arrangement-1.png)

*   **Manager**: A bilingual translator and facilitator ensuring smooth communication throughout the session
*   **Sound Engineer**: A professional audio specialist operating state-of-the-art recording equipment

The Vocal Director began by walking Amber through the studio, explaining the recording booth's acoustic design, microphone placement, and headphone mix preferences. This isn't just about comfort – it's about optimal vocal capture. K-pop studios are engineered to capture every nuance of a vocalist's performance, from breath control to subtle emotional inflections.

**Step 2: Studio Tour & Microphone Preparation**

The Manager guided Amber through a comprehensive studio orientation, including:

*   **Microphone testing** to ensure optimal sound levels and frequency response
*   **Cue box explanation** – the in-ear monitor system that K-pop artists use during recording
*   **Booth positioning** to maximize vocal resonance and minimize ambient noise

Amber practiced adjusting her monitor mix, finding the right balance between her own voice and the instrumental backing track. This preparation phase is crucial; many singers struggle initially with hearing themselves properly through headphones, but professional guidance makes all the difference.

**Step 3: Vocal Warm-Up & Technique Training**

![Vocal Director Training Session at King Studio - Seoul Experience Kpop Vocal Lesson](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2025/11/Seoul-Kpop-Experience-Vocal-Director-Training-King-Studio.png)

Before any official recording began, the Vocal Director led Amber through an intensive vocal training session. This wasn't generic warming up – it was specific K-pop vocal methodology:

*   **Breath control exercises** to maximize sustain and power
*   **Pitch accuracy drills** using the recording booth's real-time monitoring
*   **Articulation practice** to ensure clarity on every syllable of "Oceans"
*   **Emotional delivery coaching** to capture the song's spiritual depth

K-pop vocal directors train artists to think about every phrase as serving the song's emotional arc. For "Oceans," this meant understanding when to deliver intimately (versus powerfully), how to build intensity toward key moments, and how to maintain technical precision while conveying genuine emotion.

**Step 4: Parts Analysis & Rehearsal**

The Vocal Director broke down "Oceans" into distinct vocal parts:

*   **Verse sections**: Where Amber's voice carries the narrative with clarity and presence
*   **Chorus sections**: Where power, sustain, and emotional impact become paramount
*   **Bridge sections**: Where the most vocal demanding moments occur

Through rehearsal, Amber discovered her optimal key range for the song. K-pop recordings often involve slight key adjustments to match an artist's vocal strengths while maintaining the song's intended emotional resonance. The Vocal Director tested several backing track keys until finding the sweet spot where Amber could deliver both technically perfect and emotionally powerful vocals.

**Step 5: Professional Direction**

After the intensive recording period, Amber and the entire team conducted a final monitoring session, reviewing the best takes together and identifying any sections requiring re-recording. By design, this process ensures the final album meets professional broadcast standards.

> Discover Ikseon-dong: Seoul's Charming Hanok Haven

**Why Visit:**

Nestled in the heart of Jongno-gu, Seoul, Ikseon-dong is a captivating blend of tradition and modernity, making it an unmissable spot for foreign visitors seeking an authentic Korean experience. This historic neighborhood, over 100 years old, boasts the highest density of hanoks—traditional Korean houses—in the city, with around 119 preserved structures. Once a quiet residential area, it has transformed into a trendy enclave where ancient architecture meets hip cafes, boutiques, and eateries.

Wander through its narrow, winding alleys adorned with tiled roofs and wooden beams, evoking a step back in time while surrounded by urban buzz. Perfect for cafe-hopping, indulge in artisanal coffees at spots like cozy tea houses or Instagram-worthy dessert shops offering matcha lattes and Korean sweets. Foodies will love the diverse dining scene: from fusion Korean-Italian pizzas to craft beer bars and traditional hanok restaurants serving bibimbap or street snacks like hotteok.

Shop for unique souvenirs in boutique stores selling handmade jewelry, vintage clothing, or K-beauty products. As evening falls, the area lights up with vibrant nightlife—enjoy cocktails in hidden pubs or live music in converted hanoks. It's less crowded than Bukchon Hanok Village, offering a more intimate vibe.

Easily accessible via subway (Anguk Station, Exit 6, or Jonggak Station), visit in the afternoon for photos or evenings for the lively atmosphere. Wear comfortable shoes for the cobblestone paths. Ikseon-dong encapsulates Seoul's soul: where history whispers amid contemporary charm. Don't miss it on your Korean adventure!
