### Understanding "Flying Solo": The Perfect Choice for Emotional Vocal Expression

**About Julie and the Phantoms**  
Julie and the Phantoms is a Netflix musical comedy-drama series that premiered in 2020, capturing hearts globally with its themes of grief, friendship, and finding your voice through music. The show follows Julie, a teenage girl who rediscovers her passion for music after connecting with a ghost band from the 1990s.​

The series resonated particularly with young audiences for its authentic portrayal of loss, artistic vulnerability, and the transformative power of creative expression – themes that align perfectly with KING STUDIO's mission of helping people discover their vocal identity.​

**"Flying Solo" – Song Analysis**  
Musical Style: "Flying Solo" features a dreamy, atmospheric pop-rock arrangement with layered vocals, acoustic guitar foundations, and driving percussion. The song builds progressively from intimate verses to powerful, anthemic choruses.​​

**Lyrical Themes:** The song celebrates independence and self-empowerment after a relationship ends. Its message – "I can shine by myself" – resonates deeply with anyone discovering their individual strength and identity.​

*   Vocal Requirements: The song demands:
*   Range flexibility: Comfortable verses with soaring chorus moments
*   Emotional authenticity: Vulnerability transforming into confidence
*   Dynamic control: Soft, intimate passages contrasting with powerful declarations
*   Rhythmic precision: Syncopated phrasing and groove awareness

These characteristics made "Flying Solo" an excellent match for Susanne's vocal profile: she could express genuine emotion through the verses while challenging herself with the anthem-like chorus sections.​​

**Why This Song Works for K-Pop Fans**  
While "Flying Solo" isn't technically a K-Pop song, its production values, emotional depth, and vocal styling share significant DNA with contemporary K-Pop ballads and pop-rock tracks. Korean artists frequently incorporate similar atmospheric production, heartfelt storytelling, and dynamic vocal delivery.​

For international K-Pop fans like Susanne, songs that blend Western and Korean musical sensibilities offer comfortable yet exciting territory for exploring their vocal capabilities in a K-Pop production context.
