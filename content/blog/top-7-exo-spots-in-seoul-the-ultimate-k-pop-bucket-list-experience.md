### Top 7 Mabongnim Halmeoni Tteokbokki / 마복림 할머니 떡볶이

![](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2025/12/396511296_175567722274184_2478126557081582855_n.webp)

![](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2025/12/369771234_635511071714800_3462445080675174014_n.webp)

![](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2025/12/469114946_18352815160124186_6506277445243260316_n.webp)

Starting our **Seoul Travel** guide at number 7 is a spot beloved by Chanyeol. Located in the famous Sindang-dong Tteokbokki Town, this place is legendary.

*   **The 'Behind' Story:** [Chanyeol](https://www.instagram.com/real__pcy) visited this spot and shared his love for this traditional style of Tteokbokki (Spicy Rice Cakes). It’s not just street food; here, it’s a full meal cooked right at your table. It’s a must-visit for a true local **Seoul Experience**.
*   **Vibe & Menu:** It’s loud, bustling, and authentic. You must order the **Set Menu for 2 (or more)** which includes ramyeon noodles, eggs, and dumplings in the spicy sauce.
*   **Foreigner-Friendly Info:**
    *   **Location:** Sindang-dong (near Dongdaemun).
    *   **Google Maps Search:** "Mabongnim Halmeoni Tteokbokki"
    *   **Address(Google):** [5 Dasan-ro 35-gil, Jung-gu, Seoul](https://www.google.com/maps?um=1&ie=UTF-8&fb=1&gl=kr&sa=X&geocode=KU_RaOQVo3w1MfCuxK61Cbr0&daddr=%EC%84%9C%EC%9A%B8%ED%8A%B9%EB%B3%84%EC%8B%9C+%EC%A4%91%EA%B5%AC+%EC%8B%A0%EB%8B%B9%EB%8F%99+%EB%8B%A4%EC%82%B0%EB%A1%9C35%EA%B8%B8+5)
    *   **Address (Naver):** [서울 중구 다산로35길 5](https://map.naver.com/p/directions/-/14139259.071439814,4517918.908178585,%EB%A7%88%EB%B3%B5%EB%A6%BC%ED%95%A0%EB%A8%B8%EB%8B%88%EC%A7%91,38405139,PLACE_POI/-/car)
    *   **Tip:** Great for groups! The menu is simple (mostly sets), so you won’t struggle with ordering.

### Top 6 Lau Lau / 라우라우

![](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2025/12/12446101_1698433293702547_1951561765_n.webp)

![](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2025/12/16906306_1304056779679602_5057148752079355904_n.webp)

![](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2025/12/31174520_994808774000191_2970865768117305344_n.webp)

If you want to eat healthy like the maknae Sehun, head to Lau Lau in Cheongdam.

*   **The 'Behind' Story:** [Sehun](https://www.instagram.com/oohsehun) has been spotted here frequently. He is known to love their Australian-style Shabu Shabu (Hot pot). It’s a slightly more upscale spot where idols go to recharge with good protein and veggies.
*   **Vibe & Menu:** The atmosphere is calm and clean. The signature dish is the **Wagyu Beef Shabu Shabu**. The way they wrap the meat and veggies in lettuce is unique and delicious.
*   **Foreigner-Friendly Info:**
    *   **Location:** Cheongdam-dong (Gangnam).
    *   **Google Maps Search:** "Lau Lau Cheongdam"
    *   **Address(Google):** [1F, 420, Dosan-daero, Gangnam-gu, Seoul](https://www.google.com/maps?um=1&ie=UTF-8&fb=1&gl=kr&sa=X&geocode=KcMha0h2pHw1MZ4x4LBe0O2G&daddr=%EC%84%9C%EC%9A%B8%ED%8A%B9%EB%B3%84%EC%8B%9C+%EA%B0%95%EB%82%A8%EA%B5%AC+%EB%8F%84%EC%82%B0%EB%8C%80%EB%A1%9C+420+1%EC%B8%B5+121%ED%98%B8)
    *   **Address (Naver):** [서울 강남구 도산대로 420 1층 121호](https://map.naver.com/p/directions/-/14140207.8474598,4511649.9729124,%ED%95%98%EB%82%A8%EB%8F%BC%EC%A7%80%EC%A7%91%20%EA%B0%80%EB%A1%9C%EC%88%98%EA%B8%B8%EC%A0%90,1396174983,PLACE_POI/-/car?c=15.00,0,0,0,dh)
    *   **Tip:** It is popular, so try to go during off-peak hours or ask your hotel concierge to help make a reservation.

### Top 5 Hanam Pig House / 하남돼지집

![](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2025/12/484145230_18513408388032747_1536768481072081673_n.webp)

![](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2025/12/504392192_18332016277163108_4900434989981503665_n.webp)

![](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2025/12/473778436_526898273699824_5528102753478243044_n.webp)

No **Korea Travel** itinerary is complete without Korean BBQ (Samgyeopsal).

💡Cheongdam branch is closed, and the nearest Garosu-gil is guided

*   **The 'Behind' Story:** While this is a franchise, the Cheongdam branch is a hotspot for SM Entertainment artists, including [EXO](https://www.instagram.com/weareone.exo), due to its proximity to the old SM building. It’s the perfect place to experience the "after-practice dinner" vibe.
*   **Vibe & Menu:** Sizzling, savory, and energetic. The staff cuts and grills the meat for you (very convenient!). Order the **Samgyeopsal (Pork Belly)** and **Moksal (Pork Neck)**.
*   **Foreigner-Friendly Info:**
    *   **Location:** Cheongdam-dong.
    *   **Google Maps Search:** "Hanam Pig House Garosu-gil branch"
    *   **Address(Google):** [1F Taeyoung Building, 55 Gangnam-daero 152-gil, Gangnam-gu, Seoul](https://www.google.com/maps?gs_lcrp=EgZjaHJvbWUyBggAEEUYOTIKCAEQABiiBBiJBTIHCAIQABjvBTIKCAMQABiABBiiBDIHCAQQABjvBTIKCAUQABiiBBiJBdIBCDY1ODJqMGo3qAIIsAIB8QWtQUkdIXMK9vEFrUFJHSFzCvY&um=1&ie=UTF-8&fb=1&gl=kr&sa=X&geocode=KVHINgt_o3w1MZ3dIRHFyPT7&daddr=%EC%84%9C%EC%9A%B8%ED%8A%B9%EB%B3%84%EC%8B%9C+%EA%B0%95%EB%82%A8%EA%B5%AC+%EC%8B%A0%EC%82%AC%EB%8F%99+%EA%B0%95%EB%82%A8%EB%8C%80%EB%A1%9C152%EA%B8%B8+55)
    *   **Address (Naver):** [서울 강남구 강남대로152길 55 태영빌딩 1층](https://map.naver.com/p/directions/-/14140207.8474598,4511649.9729124,%ED%95%98%EB%82%A8%EB%8F%BC%EC%A7%80%EC%A7%91%20%EA%B0%80%EB%A1%9C%EC%88%98%EA%B8%B8%EC%A0%90,1396174983,PLACE_POI/-/car?c=15.00,0,0,0,dh)
    *   **Tip:** Very foreigner-friendly service. They are used to international fans!

### Top 4 Our Bakery(Apgujeong) / 아우어베이커리

![](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2025/12/163300773_468795234477093_882418631420829646_n.webp)

![](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2025/12/338597820_170566932479475_1417181699204569901_n.webp)

![](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2025/12/120808653_805008866994573_1044413636425126819_n.webp)

Need a caffeine fix during your **KPop Trip**? This trendy bakery is a favorite among K-Pop stylists and idols, including EXO members.

*   **The 'Behind' Story:** Known for its Instagrammable aesthetics and high-quality pastries. Fans have spotted members here grabbing coffee or snacks. It represents the modern, chic side of the **Seoul Experience**.
*   **Vibe & Menu:** Industrial chic and smelling of fresh butter. The absolute must-try is the **"Dirty Choco"** (a chocolate croissant that makes your hands messy but happy) and their **Green Tea Latte**.
*   **Foreigner-Friendly Info:**
    *   **Location:** Apgujeong-ro.
    *   **Google Maps Search:** "Our Bakery Apgujeong"
    *   **Address(Google):** [19, Apgujeong-ro 79-gil, Gangnam-gu, Seoul](https://www.google.com/maps/dir//%EC%84%9C%EC%9A%B8%ED%8A%B9%EB%B3%84%EC%8B%9C+%EA%B0%95%EB%82%A8%EA%B5%AC+%EC%95%95%EA%B5%AC%EC%A0%95%EB%A1%9C79%EA%B8%B8+19/data=!4m6!4m5!1m1!4e2!1m2!1m1!1s0x357ca47b1190dcef:0xfa7b8425fb88126e?sa=X&ved=1t:707&ictx=111)
    *   **Address (Naver):** [서울 강남구 압구정로79길 19](https://map.naver.com/p/directions/-/14143015.9706707,4512578.2764293,%EC%95%84%EC%9A%B0%EC%96%B4%EB%B2%A0%EC%9D%B4%EC%BB%A4%EB%A6%AC%20%EC%B2%AD%EB%8B%B4%EC%82%AC%EA%B1%B0%EB%A6%AC%EC%A0%90,1913182999,PLACE_POI/-/car?c=15.00,0,0,0,dh)
    *   **Tip:** Perfect for solo travelers to relax. English ordering is generally easy here.

### Top 3 Gold Pig Dining(Geumdwaeji Sikdang) / 금돼지식당

![](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2025/12/490300593_18508797583003866_7635414726535870690_n.webp)

![](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2025/12/493726716_18302466583209902_7843528485140280422_n.webp)

![](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2025/12/487473068_18385310173119275_1039763354286311538_n.webp)

Entering the Top 3 is a Michelin Guide restaurant that Suho and Sehun love!

*   **The 'Behind' Story:** This isn't just any BBQ place; it's the place. "Gold Pig Dining" is famous for its distinct white tiled exterior and premium pork. EXO members (and even BTS members) are known regulars here. It’s a certified **K-Pop experience** culinary hotspot.
*   **Vibe & Menu:** Hip, crowded, and premium. The **Bone-in Pork Belly (Bon-Samgyeopsal)** is the star here. The texture is unlike anything you've tasted before.
*   **Foreigner-Friendly Info:**
    *   **Location:** Sindang-dong.
    *   **Google Maps Search:** "Geumdwaeji Sikdang"
    *   **Address(Google):** [149 Dasan-ro, Jung-gu, Seoul](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/37.5062528,127.0808576/%EC%84%9C%EC%9A%B8%ED%8A%B9%EB%B3%84%EC%8B%9C+%EC%A4%91%EA%B5%AC+%EB%8B%A4%EC%82%B0%EB%A1%9C+149/data=!3m1!4b1!4m9!4m8!1m1!4e1!1m5!1m1!1s0x357ca3110d84285f:0x28f49b1739f72531!2m2!1d127.0116712!2d37.5570994?entry=ttu&g_ep=EgoyMDI1MTIwNy4wIKXMDSoASAFQAw%3D%3D)
    *   **Address (Naver):** [서울 중구 다산로 149](https://map.naver.com/p/directions/-/14138877.50162122,4517045.499409623,%EA%B8%88%EB%8F%BC%EC%A7%80%EC%8B%9D%EB%8B%B9,37869877,PLACE_POI/-/car?c=15.00,0,0,0,dh)
    *   **Tip:** The wait times can be very long (1-2 hours). Go early! English menus are usually available.

### Top2 Cofioca / 커피오카

![](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2025/12/70538312_496064074309558_6972027583863909516_n.webp)

![](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2025/12/22157781_350879122022625_3108728042155933696_n.webp)

![](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2025/12/70144258_724238038076974_4800796613398107663_n.webp)

The runner-up is a place steeped in SM Entertainment history.

*   **The 'Behind' Story:** Located in Apgujeong, this tiny bubble tea shop is covered in autographs from K-Pop stars. [Sehun](https://www.instagram.com/oohsehun) is their most famous patron; he has been coming here since his trainee days. The owner often shares stories about Sehun’s visits with fans!
*   **Vibe & Menu:** Cozy, nostalgic, and filled with fan love. You are literally sitting in history. Order the **Chocolate Colada** or **Honeydew Melon Milk Tea** (Sehun’s favorites).
*   **Foreigner-Friendly Info:**
    *   **Location:** Apgujeong rodeo area.
    *   **Google Maps Search:** "Cofioca"
    *   **Address(Google):** [31, Seonneung-ro 161-gil, Gangnam-gu, Seoul](https://www.google.com/maps/dir//%EC%84%9C%EC%9A%B8%ED%8A%B9%EB%B3%84%EC%8B%9C+%EA%B0%95%EB%82%A8%EA%B5%AC+%EC%84%A0%EB%A6%89%EB%A1%9C161%EA%B8%B8+31/data=!4m6!4m5!1m1!4e2!1m2!1m1!1s0x357ca387b6f83a57:0x3aeeb9a981e5617a?sa=X&ved=1t:707&ictx=111)
    *   **Address (Naver):** [서울특별시 강남구 선릉로161길 31](https://map.naver.com/p/directions/-/14141790.7690968,4512896.287345,%EC%84%9C%EC%9A%B8%20%EA%B0%95%EB%82%A8%EA%B5%AC%20%EC%84%A0%EB%A6%89%EB%A1%9C161%EA%B8%B8%2031,,ADDRESS_POI/-/transit?c=14.00,0,0,0,dh)
    *   **Tip:** Look for EXO signatures on the wall! It’s a very small space, mostly for takeout.

### Top 1 KAMONG / 카몽

![](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2025/12/20481950_321969364920867_2339019969422950400_n.webp)

![](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2025/12/469203999_18473332672035830_1511279188147384748_n.webp)

![](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2025/12/447135769_7327779187345092_9204623532274498165_n.webp)

The Number 1 spot on our EXO **Korea Experience** list is none other than **KAMONG**.

*   **The 'Behind' Story:** The name "KAMONG" comes from [**KA**i](https://www.instagram.com/zkdlin) + **MONG**gu (his dog). This café is owned by Kai’s sister! It is the holy grail for EXO-Ls. Kai himself visits occasionally, and the cafe is practically a museum of [EXO](https://www.instagram.com/weareone.exo) love tailored by his family.
*   **Vibe & Menu:** Modern, sleek, and spacious with high ceilings. The waffles here are legendary. You must order the **Ice Cream Waffle** and a hand-drip coffee.
*   **Foreigner-Friendly Info:**
    *   **Location:** Near Gangnam Station (Seocho-dong).
    *   **Google Maps Search:** "Kamong Espresso"
    *   **Address(Google):** [1F 17, Seocho-daero 42-gil, Seocho-gu, Seoul](https://www.google.com/maps/dir//Kamong+1%EC%B8%B5+17+Seocho-daero+42-gil+Seocho+District,+Seoul/data=!4m8!4m7!1m0!1m5!1m1!1s0x357ca142c95577e3:0x8494eade6018b160!2m2!1d127.0089262!2d37.4912233?entry=ttu&g_ep=EgoyMDI1MTIwNy4wIKXMDSoASAFQAw%3D%3D)
    *   **Address (Naver):** [서울 서초구 서초대로42길 17 1층](https://map.naver.com/p/directions/-/14138567.332124,4507796.1734259,%EC%B9%B4%EB%AA%BD,35075023,PLACE_POI/-/transit?c=15.00,0,0,0,dh)
    *   **Tip:** Excellent spot for laptop work or writing postcards. The staff is very kind to international fans.
