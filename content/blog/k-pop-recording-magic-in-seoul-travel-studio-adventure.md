### Song Spotlight: “Don’t Need Your Love”

**Release Background**  
The track dropped 2019-06-06 as SM STATION 3’s 13th single. It marked NCT DREAM’s first Western collaboration and HRVY’s Korean debut.

**Sound & Genre**  
A synth-pop backbone merges with bright acoustic-guitar riffs and a snappy 100 BPM beat. The bilingual verses give HRVY the English hook while NCT DREAM tackle Korean rap lines.

**Lyric & MV Theme**  
The chorus declares total independence from a past relationship—“I don’t need your love”—while the MV casts the artists as the DNYL club on a school campus, dancing off heartbreak.

**Why the Collab Matters**  
Cross-continental projects like BTS × Halsey or BLACKPINK × Dua Lipa showed K-pop’s global pull; NCT DREAM × HRVY continued that momentum, landing BBC Radio 1’s Brit List nod for HRVY the same year.

**HRVY at a Glance**  
Harvey Leigh Cantwell, 178cm and 57kg, debuted in 2013 with “Thank You,” hosted CBBC’s “Friday Download,” and later amassed massive TikTok views. Fans dub him “NCT DREAM’s honorary 7th member”.

### Nearby Seoul Travel Gems
