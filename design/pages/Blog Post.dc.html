Mia landed in Seoul with a shortlist of three songs and a lot of nerves. She had never recorded anything — not even a voice memo she was willing to keep. What she wanted was simple: to stand in a real booth and hear her own voice on a track she loved.

Choosing the song

We talked through range and tempo before anything else. The chorus she was worried about turned out to be the easiest part once the producer broke it into phrases. Picking a song you genuinely enjoy matters more than picking the "impressive" one — you will sing it better.

"I stopped thinking about being good and started just enjoying it. That is when the takes got better."

In the booth

Vocal directing is the whole experience. Take after take, the producer shaped tone and timing — never rushing, always guiding. There is no separate "pick your song" or "record" step; the directing and the recording happen together, until it feels right to you.

Taking it home

Mia booked Diamond, so a few weeks after her session the mixed and mastered track arrived by secure link, along with a custom CD. She left the studio the same day with her recording in hand, and the polished version followed once post-production wrapped.

She flew home with a track that sounds like her — at her best.
